📣 Executive Summary — what happenedDemocrats swept Bucks County and Harrisburg. Bob Harvie (D) ousted five-term Republican Brian Fitzpatrick in PA-1 — the AP called it at 12:41 a.m. Wednesday — flipping a seat the GOP held for more than a decade. Democrats flipped the Pennsylvania state Senate for the first time in over 30 years (locally, Eileen Hartnett Albillar unseated Sen. Frank Farry in SD-6 and Mark Pinsley unseated Sen. Jarrett Coleman in SD-16), and retained the state House, giving the party a full Harrisburg “trifecta” with Gov. Josh Shapiro’s re-election. In the state House, Democrats flipped HD-18 (Bryan Allen), HD-142 (Kristin Egan) and, by a razor-thin margin, HD-143 (Tim Hayes). Republicans held HD-145 (Craig Staats) and HD-178 (Kristin Marcell). Results remain unofficial pending certification; HD-143 was still extremely close with the incumbent not yet conceding.
