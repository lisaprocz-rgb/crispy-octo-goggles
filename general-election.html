📊 Executive Summary — what happened: Bucks County Democrats swept the 2026 general election. Bob Harvie (D) ousted five-term Rep. Brian Fitzpatrick (R) in PA-1, the first Democrat to represent Bucks County in Congress since 2011. In the state Senate, Eileen Hartnett Albillar flipped SD-6 (defeating incumbent Frank Farry) and Mark Pinsley flipped SD-16 (defeating incumbent Jarrett Coleman) — part of Democrats flipping the Pennsylvania state Senate for the first time since 1993 and, with Gov. Josh Shapiro's reelection and a held/expanded state House majority, securing a Democratic "trifecta" in Harrisburg for the first time in roughly three decades. Locally, Democrats flipped three state House seats — HD-18 (Bryan Allen), HD-142 (Kristin Egan) and HD-143 (Tim Hayes) — pushing the Bucks delegation to 8 Democrats and 2 Republicans. Republicans held HD-145 and HD-178.
