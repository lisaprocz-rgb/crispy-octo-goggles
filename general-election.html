Executive summary (new today): A Democratic "blue wave" swept Bucks County. Commissioner Bob Harvie (D) ousted five-term Rep. Brian Fitzpatrick (R) in PA-1 — a major upset the AP called at 12:41 a.m. Nov. 4 — part of a Democratic sweep of Pennsylvania's competitive U.S. House seats. Democrats also flipped the state Senate for the first time since 1978, giving Gov. Josh Shapiro a Harrisburg trifecta; Bucks-area flips included Eileen Hartnett Albillar (SD-6) over Sen. Frank Farry and Mark Pinsley (SD-16) over Sen. Jarrett Coleman. In the state House, Democrats flipped HD-18 (Bryan Allen) and HD-142 (Kristin Egan) and lead the still-uncalled HD-143 by 180 votes (Tim Hayes), while holding every Democratic incumbent seat. Republicans held HD-145 (Craig Staats) and HD-178 (Kristin Marcell). Countywide turnout was about 62.7% (≈305,378 of 487,385 registered). All results remain unofficial pending certification.
Ballot correction (per STEP 0 verification): In HD-145, Democratic nominee Les Mavus withdrew before the election. The contest was ultimately Rep. Craig Staats (R) vs. independent Rebecca Prendergast (a former Republican); there was no Democrat on the ballot. All other matchups matched the certified candidate list.
