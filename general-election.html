Executive summary — a Democratic sweep. Bucks County powered a banner night for Pennsylvania Democrats. Bob Harvie (D) unseated five-term Rep. Brian Fitzpatrick (R) in PA-1, a race the AP called and one of four PA swing U.S. House seats Democrats flipped. Democrats also flipped the Pennsylvania state Senate for the first time in more than three decades — Bucks-area wins by Eileen Hartnett Albillar (SD-6) and Mark Pinsley (SD-16) were part of that takeover — and expanded their state House majority, handing Gov. Josh Shapiro (re-elected) a Democratic trifecta in Harrisburg. In the Bucks state House delegation, Democrats flipped HD-18 (Bryan Allen) and HD-142 (Kristin Egan), and Tim Hayes holds a narrow, still-uncalled lead in HD-143. Republicans held HD-145 (Craig Staats) and HD-178 (Kristin Marcell); every Democratic incumbent held.
