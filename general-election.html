📝 Executive Summary. With two days to go, the marquee PA-1 race (Harvie vs. Fitzpatrick) is a genuine dead heat — the only public poll (Inquirer/NYT/Siena) puts Harvie up 48–46, and both Cook Political Report and Sabato's Crystal Ball have moved the seat to toss-up. The final-weekend blitz was all-out: Gov. Shapiro closed a 10-day statewide bus tour Saturday (Oct 31) with a Doylestown rally for Harvie, while Fitzpatrick's team said it knocked 3,000 doors and Trump's TeleRally for the GOP ticket pointedly left Fitzpatrick off the endorsement list. Control of the state Senate may run through Bucks: SD-6 (Albillar/Farry) and SD-16 (Pinsley/Coleman) are both polling as statistical ties, and Bob Casey stumped Oct 27 in Langhorne for Albillar and state-House candidate Kristin Egan. As of Thursday, Pennsylvania Democrats had returned roughly 629,000 mail ballots to Republicans' 243,000 statewide.
