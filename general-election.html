Executive Summary

The marquee race — PA-1, Bob Harvie (D) vs. Rep. Brian Fitzpatrick (R) — has tightened into a genuine toss-up in the final stretch. A new Inquirer/New York Times/Siena poll (fielded Oct 16–22) puts Harvie ahead 48%–46%, inside the margin of error, and forecasters have shifted the seat toward Democrats: Sabato's Crystal Ball moved it to "toss up" and Cook moved it to "leans Republican." National Democrats (Gov. Shapiro, House Minority Leader Hakeem Jeffries) have rallied in Bucks for Harvie, while Fitzpatrick keeps a large cash advantage and has sued to remove Democratic election-board commissioner Diane Ellis-Marseglia days before the vote. Down-ballot, the PA Senate and House majorities hang on a handful of Bucks seats — SD-6 (Farry/Albillar), HD-144 (Munroe/Murphy), HD-142 (Hogan/Egan) and HD-18 (Tomlinson/Allen) — but those contests drew no fresh dedicated coverage in the past 48 hours; see Key Races to Watch for the latest balance-of-power reporting.
