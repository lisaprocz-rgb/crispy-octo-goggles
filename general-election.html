📊 Executive Summary. Bucks County powered a Democratic sweep. Bob Harvie unseated five-term Rep. Brian Fitzpatrick in PA-01 — the night's biggest upset — as Democrats flipped four Pennsylvania U.S. House seats. In the state Senate, Eileen Hartnett Albillar ousted Sen. Frank Farry (SD-6) and Mark Pinsley declared victory over Sen. Jarrett Coleman (SD-16), helping Democrats flip the chamber for the first time in decades and complete a Harrisburg trifecta alongside re-elected Gov. Josh Shapiro. In the state House, Bryan Allen (HD-18) and Kristin Egan (HD-142) flipped Republican-held Bucks seats, while Democratic incumbents Steve Santarsiero (SD-10) and Brian Munroe (HD-144) held on. The Bucks County Beacon's results hub tallies two Bucks state House flips and one state Senate flip red-to-blue; several down-ballot House races were still being finalized at this run.
