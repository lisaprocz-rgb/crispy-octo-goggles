🎬 Cannes Film Festival 2026 Opens — A Cannes Without Hollywood
The 79th Festival de Cannes kicked off yesterday (May 12) and runs through May 23. Pierre Salvadori's The Electric Kiss served as the opening-night film, with Park Chan-wook as jury president. The major story: a near-total absence of Hollywood studio films — this year's lineup leans heavily on indies and international auteurs. Demi Moore, Jane Fonda, Heidi Klum, Maura Higgins, James Franco and Joan Collins walked the opening-night red carpet. Guillermo del Toro's Pan's Labyrinth got a special pre-opening screening, returning two decades after earning the festival's longest standing ovation (22 minutes).

The basketball world is mourning Jason Collins, who died Tuesday May 12 at age 47. His family announced his passing in a statement. Collins made history in 2013 as the first openly gay active player in any of the four major North American men's pro sports leagues.

Kelvin Evans, 40, pleaded guilty May 12 in Fulton County Superior Court in Atlanta to entering an automobile with intent to commit theft and criminal trespass.
