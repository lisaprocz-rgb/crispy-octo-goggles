🍿 Spider-Man: Brand New DayPG-13 · 2 hr 24 minFighting crime full-time as Spider-Man in a world that doesn't remember him — and the pressure of seeing his old friends move on without him — sparks a change in Peter Parker he may not have the power to control. But that transformation might also be the only thing that can stop a shocking new threat to the city: a powerful villain no one can even see. Starring Tom Holland, Zendaya and Sadie Sink.
🍿 The OdysseyR · 2 hr 52 minOdysseus, the legendary King of Ithaca, embarks on a long and perilous journey home following the Trojan War, confronting the whims of gods, mythological monsters, and trials that stretch both his cunning and his humanity to the breaking point. Christopher Nolan directs Matt Damon, Tom Holland, Anne Hathaway, Robert Pattinson and Charlize Theron.
🍿 Insidious: Out Of The FurtherPG-13 · 1 hr 46 minAmelia Eve stars as Gemma, a young mother who discovers she can travel into The Further, the purgatorial realm of lost souls at the heart of the Insidious universe. When something evil comes after her, Gemma discovers she doesn't just enter The Further — she can bring what lives there back to the real world. Once the demons realize her power, our world becomes their playground.
🍿 Toy Story 5PG · 1 hr 42 minThe age of toys is over…? Woody, Buzz and the gang return in Pixar's latest chapter, with Tom Hanks, Tim Allen and Joan Cusack.
🍿 The End of Oak StreetPG-13 · 1 hr 39 minAfter a mysterious cosmic event rips Oak Street from suburbia and transports their neighborhood to someplace unknown, the Platt family soon discovers that their survival depends on sticking together as they navigate their now unrecognizable surroundings. Anne Hathaway and Ewan McGregor star; David Robert Mitchell directs, J.J. Abrams produces.
🍿 TonyR · 1 hr 46 minA 19-year-old Anthony Bourdain travels to Provincetown and stumbles into the chaotic world of a restaurant kitchen, setting off a summer that will shape the course of his life. Dominic Sessa, Antonio Banderas and Emilia Jones star.
🍿 Paw Patrol: The Dino MoviePG · 1 hr 28 minThe PAW Patrol crash land on an uncharted tropical island filled with dinosaurs and meet Rex, a pup stranded for years who's become an expert in all things dino. When Mayor Humdinger's reckless mining wakes a dormant volcano, the pups face dino-sized rescues bigger than anything they've done before.
🍿 American Martyr: The Stanley Rother StoryNR · 1 hr 55 minA feature-length documentary narrated by Martin Sheen telling the true story of Father Stanley Rother, an Oklahoma-born priest who served the Indigenous Tz'utujil people of Santiago Atitlán, Guatemala during the country's brutal civil war — and was murdered for refusing to abandon his people.
