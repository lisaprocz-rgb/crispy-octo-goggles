📰 Broadway News & Buzz
🏆 2026 Tony Awards — June 779th annual ceremony at Radio City Music Hall, broadcast live on CBS & streaming on Paramount+. P!nk hosts. Nominations were unveiled May 5 by Uzo Aduba and Darren Criss.
🏆 Tony Nomination LeadersThe Lost Boys and Schmigadoon! each pulled 12 nominations, with Ragtime close behind at 11. Best Musical race is wide open with Titanique and Two Strangers (Carry a Cake Across New York) also in contention. Record-setting nods for Danny Burstein and June Squibb.
Maybe Happy Ending: Darren Criss says goodbyeCriss plays his final performance as Oliver tonight (May 17). Zachary Noah Piser (KPOP, Dear Evan Hansen) and Hannah Kevitt take over as Oliver and Claire starting May 19.
Every Brilliant Thing: Mariska Hargitay's Broadway debutDaniel Radcliffe finishes his run on May 24. SVU's Mariska Hargitay steps in for a four-week stint May 26 – June 28 in this solo show about loss, love, and lists.
Celebrity Autobiography opens this weekPreviews began May 16; official opening May 18 at the Shubert. The rotating cast includes Matthew Broderick, Katie Couric, Anthony Anderson, Jason Alexander, Tiler Peck, and Leslie Rodriguez Kritzer.
Just In Time closes May 29The immersive Bobby Darin musical takes its final bow at Circle in the Square after a strong run. Isa Briones plays her final performance as Connie Francis on closing night.
Spring closings stack upFallen Angels (June 7), Becky Shaw (June 14), The Balusters (June 21), Every Brilliant Thing (July 5), and Dog Day Afternoon (July 12) all wrap before mid-summer.
Oh, Mary! enters a new chapterMaya Rudolph's record-breaking run as Mary Todd Lincoln ended last summer; the show continues at the Lyceum with a rotating roster of stars stepping into Cole Escola's signature role.
2026–27 Season is loading upStar wattage incoming: Tom Hiddleston, Hayley Atwell, Rosamund Pike, Raúl Esparza, Laura Linney, Rachel Zegler, and Billy Crystal all return to Broadway between October 2026 and Spring 2027.
