📰 Broadway News & Buzz
🏆 2026 Tony nominations announced May 5The Lost Boys and Schmigadoon! lead the field with 12 nods each; Ragtime follows with 11. Best Musical race: The Lost Boys, Schmigadoon!, Titaníque, Two Strangers (Carry a Cake Across New York). Best Play: The Balusters, Giant, Liberation, Little Bear Ridge Road.
🏆 Pink to host the 79th Tony Awards June 7Live from Radio City Music Hall on CBS & Paramount+, 8–11 p.m. ET. Pink breaks the tradition of having a Broadway performer host: "It is the honor of an entire lifetime."
🔥 Eugene O'Neill Theatre fire halts Book of MormonA 3-alarm rooftop electrical fire May 4 caused major damage including holes in the roof. NYC issued a Full Vacate Order; performances cancelled through May 17. One firefighter sustained minor injuries; theatre was empty at the time.
🎭 Maybe Happy Ending: Darren Criss says goodbye May 17Tony winner Criss plays his final performance May 17; Zachary Noah Piser & Hannah Kevitt assume the leads May 19. He was the last original-cast holdout.
🎤 JoJo joins Chess on BroadwayPop singer JoJo Levesque takes over as Florence Vassy June 23, replacing Lea Michele (final perf June 21). Chess closes Sept 13.
🎭 Six's original Aragon, Adrianna Hicks, departs May 17Hicks originated the role of Catherine of Aragon on Broadway; she'll play her final performance after a long-celebrated run.
🎭 MJ original star Tavon Olds-Sample exits May 31After more than 2,000 performances as Michael, Olds-Sample departs the Neil Simon Theatre at month's end.
📈 Schmigadoon! extends through January 3, 2027A 17-week extension at the Nederlander after a leading 12 Tony nominations, including Best Musical.
📈 Operation Mincemeat extended a sixth timeThe London hit will now run at the John Golden through July 5, 2026, well past its original 16-week limit.
🎟️ Spring & summer closings stack upThe Balusters & Fallen Angels close June 7; Becky Shaw June 14; Every Brilliant Thing & Giant June 28; Operation Mincemeat July 5; Dog Day Afternoon & The Fear of 13 July 12; Proof July 19; Joe Turner's Come and Gone July 26; Ragtime Aug 2; Death of a Salesman Aug 9.
