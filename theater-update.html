📰 Broadway News & Buzz
🏆 2026 Tony nominations announced May 5Uzo Aduba & Darren Criss revealed the field on CBS Mornings; Schmigadoon! and The Lost Boys lead with 12 noms each, Ragtime follows with 11, Death of a Salesman leads plays with 9.
🏆 Tony Awards ceremony June 7 at Radio City Music HallPink hosts the 79th Tonys live on CBS & Paramount+, 8–11 p.m. ET. Best Musical race: The Lost Boys, Schmigadoon!, Titaníque, Two Strangers (Carry a Cake Across New York).
🔥 Eugene O'Neill Theatre fire halts Book of MormonA 3-alarm electrical fire May 4 caused major damage to the lighting room. NYC issued a Full Vacate Order; performances cancelled through May 17 with a return "anticipated in the coming weeks."
🎭 Maybe Happy Ending: Darren Criss says farewellTony winner Criss plays his final performance May 17 alongside Helen J Shen; Zachary Noah Piser & Hannah Kevitt assume the leads May 19.
📈 Schmigadoon! extends through January 3, 2027A 17-week extension at the Nederlander after 12 Tony nominations.
📈 Operation Mincemeat extended a sixth timeThe London hit will now run at the John Golden through July 5, 2026, well past its original 16-week limit.
🎤 JoJo joins Chess on BroadwayPop singer JoJo Levesque takes over as Florence Vassy June 23 through closing September 13, replacing Lea Michele (final perf June 21).
🏆 Tiny Desk meets BroadwayRagtime's Joshua Henry, Caissie Levy, Brandon Uranowitz & co. performed five songs on NPR's Tiny Desk this week as Tony eligibility wrapped.
🎟️ Spring closings are stacking upThe Balusters and Fallen Angels both close June 7; Becky Shaw June 14; Every Brilliant Thing & Giant June 28; Operation Mincemeat & Dog Day Afternoon & The Fear of 13 in July; Death of a Salesman Aug 9.
🎭 Tony nominees Titaníque & Two Strangers celebrateBoth first-time nominees threw cast parties this week — Titaníque earned 4 nods (incl. Best Musical), the West End import Two Strangers is now in the Best Musical race.
