Executive Summary
PA-01 Democrats Bob Harvie and Lucia Simonelli held their first head-to-head forum Monday night in Doylestown, packing a Methodist church for nearly two hours of contrasts on healthcare, Trump, and party direction. Across the river in the Lehigh Valley, the 16th Senate District's two Democrats — Mark Pinsley and Bradley Merkl-Gump — debated tonight at the Univest Public Media Center in Bethlehem. PECO formally withdrew its proposed double-digit rate hike late last week, vindicating Sen. Steve Santarsiero and the Bucks delegation that pushed back hard in early April.

U.S. Congress — 1st Congressional District

Bob Harvie — Falls Township

Bucks County Commissioner who announced his congressional bid in April 2025. A lifelong Bucks County resident born in Bristol Borough, Harvie graduated magna cum laude from George Washington University and spent 20 years as a teacher and Social Studies Department chair at Bucks County Technical High School. Elected County Commissioner in 2019, named board chair in 2022, re-elected in 2023. His campaign centers on economic fairness and kitchen-table issues.

Lucia Simonelli — Quakertown Borough

A PhD mathematician and former scientific advisor in the U.S. Senate. Born in Lansdale, now settled in Quakertown, she directs programs for a nonprofit focused on scaling clean-air technologies. Running as a progressive zeroed in on universal healthcare and Medicare for All.

Pennsylvania State Senate

Steve Santarsiero — 10th Senatorial District (Incumbent)

Has represented the 10th District since 2019 and serves as chair of the Bucks County Democrats. Running unopposed in the May primary. Active on minimum wage, PECO rate increases, and other legislative issues.

Wiss Now — PECO withdraws proposed rate hike that would have raised monthly bills (Apr 20, 2026)
PECO formally pulled its proposed double-digit electricity and gas rate increases — a win for the southeast PA legislative delegation, including Santarsiero, that publicly opposed the filing earlier this month.
PA Senate Democrats — State Senator Steve Santarsiero Statement on PECO's Proposed Rate Increase (Apr 1, 2026)
Santarsiero blasted PECO's then-pending 12.5% electricity and 11.4% gas hikes as out of touch with a Bucks County cost-of-living crisis, and pressed Senate leadership to take up the Governor's Lightning Plan and HB1834 to shift data-center grid costs to developers.

Eileen Hartnett Albillar — 6th Senatorial District

Bucks County Clerk of Courts who launched her bid in January 2026 to unseat Republican Frank Farry. A former Warrington Township supervisor with a background in social work and nonprofit leadership. Endorsed by Senate Democratic Leader Jay Costa, Sen. Santarsiero, Commissioners Harvie and Ellis-Marseglia. Focuses on affordability, SEPTA funding, and working families.
