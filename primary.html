Bucks County 2026 Democratic Primary — Daily Media Scan

April 20, 2026

Today's Roundup: With 29 days until the May 19 primary, attention is converging on the PA-01 congressional race between Bucks County Commissioner Bob Harvie and Quakertown progressive Lucia Simonelli. The League of Women Voters has announced a candidate forum for April 27, and Harvie continues to roll out establishment endorsements while Simonelli presses a Medicare for All-focused progressive lane. State House candidate Kristin Egan (HD-142) drew coverage this month for going on record against the $227M PennDOT US-1 reconstruction. Senate Democrats continue to focus on the 6th (Albillar vs. Farry) and the contested 16th (Pinsley vs. Merkl-Gump) as key flip opportunities.

U.S. Congress — 1st Congressional District

Bob Harvie

Falls Township · PA-01

Bucks County Commissioner who announced his congressional bid in April 2025. Lifelong Bucks County resident born in Bristol Borough, graduated magna cum laude from George Washington University, spent 20 years as a teacher and Social Studies Department chair at Bucks County Technical High School. Elected County Commissioner in 2019, named board chair in 2022, re-elected in 2023. Campaign centers on economic fairness and kitchen-table issues.

WHYY — "Pa. election 2026: Meet Bob Harvie and Lucia Simonelli, Dems fighting to unseat Brian Fitzpatrick" (April 2026)

Primary voter guide profiling both Democratic candidates heading into the May 19 primary. Harvie is emphasizing his Bucks roots and his platform of a $15 minimum wage, affordable housing, and lowering healthcare costs; the piece notes his broad establishment endorsement slate including Lt. Gov. Austin Davis and U.S. Reps. Madeleine Dean and Chris Deluzio.

Philadelphia Inquirer — "Meet Bob Harvie, Democrats' best chance at defeating Brian Fitzpatrick and helping win back the House" (Late March 2026)

In-depth profile casting Harvie as the Democratic establishment's preferred vehicle for flipping PA-01, a seat the DCCC has placed on its "Districts in Play" list as one of 44 top targets nationally.

Bucks County Herald — "National Democrats target PA-01 as flippable" (April 2026)

Coverage of the DCCC's continued targeting of Fitzpatrick's seat, noting this race is among four Pennsylvania contests likely to determine control of Congress in 2026.

Lucia Simonelli

Quakertown Borough · PA-01

Holds a PhD in mathematics and has served as a scientific advisor in the U.S. Senate. Born in Lansdale, now settled in Quakertown. Directs programs for a nonprofit focused on scaling clean-air technologies. Running as a progressive, zeroing in on universal healthcare and Medicare for All.

WHYY — "Pa. election 2026: Meet Bob Harvie and Lucia Simonelli, Dems fighting to unseat Brian Fitzpatrick" (April 2026)

Profile highlighting Simonelli's progressive lane — Medicare for All, climate policy, and abolishing ICE — and her outsider credentials as a mathematician and former Senate scientific advisor running a grassroots campaign from Quakertown.

Bucks County Beacon — "INTERVIEW: Could Progressive Congressional Candidate Lucia Simonelli Shock PA-01 With a Victory in the May Democratic Primary?" (February 2026, still circulating)

Long-form Q&A exploring Simonelli's pitch to progressive primary voters and her theory of the case for an upset over Harvie despite the establishment's overwhelming endorsement support.

Eileen Hartnett Albillar

6th Senatorial District · Challenging Sen. Frank Farry (R)

Bucks County Clerk of Courts who launched her bid in January 2026 to unseat Republican Frank Farry. Former Warrington Township supervisor with a background in social work and nonprofit leadership. Endorsed by Senate Democratic Leader Jay Costa, Sen. Santarsiero, Commissioners Harvie and Ellis-Marseglia. Focuses on affordability, SEPTA funding, and working families.

In-depth launch profile framing Albillar's 25 years in social work, government, and nonprofit leadership, her tenure as Warrington supervisor (preserving open space, building a police station), and her 2023 election as Clerk of Courts as the foundation for her Senate bid.

16th Senatorial District — Contested Primary

Mark Pinsley (Lehigh County Controller) vs. Bradley Merkl-Gump (Pennridge School Board)

A key race for Democrats hoping to flip the state Senate. Pinsley, Lehigh County Controller, is making his third run at the district; Merkl-Gump is a 39-year-old Perkasie resident and eighth-grade social studies teacher in the Perkiomen School District. Winner faces Republican incumbent Sen. Jarrett Coleman in November.

Bucks County Herald — "Contested Democratic primary set in Pennsylvania's 16th Senatorial District" (April 2026)

Overview of the two-way Democratic primary after a third candidate's withdrawal; positions Pinsley's long race record against Merkl-Gump's grassroots-educator profile heading into a general election against Coleman.
