U.S. Congress
Bob Harvie (D) vs. Rep. Brian Fitzpatrick (R, incumbent) — 1st Congressional District Harvie is a Bucks County Commissioner from Falls Township who announced his congressional bid in April 2025. Born in Bristol Borough, he graduated magna cum laude from George Washington University and spent 20 years teaching, chairing the Social Studies Department at Bucks County Technical High School. Elected Commissioner in 2019, named board chair in 2022 and re-elected in 2023, he won the May Democratic primary roughly 65%–35% and sits on the DCCC's "Red to Blue" list. He faces five-term Republican incumbent Brian Fitzpatrick in a race Cook rates Likely Republican — the toughest of Pennsylvania's Democratic targets, though Harris narrowly carried the district in 2024.
