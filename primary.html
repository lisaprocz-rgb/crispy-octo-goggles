U.S. Congress
Bob Harvie (D) vs. Rep. Brian Fitzpatrick (R, incumbent) — 1st Congressional District Harvie is a Bucks County Commissioner who announced his congressional bid in April 2025. A lifelong Bucks resident born in Bristol Borough, he graduated magna cum laude from George Washington University and spent 20 years as a teacher and Social Studies Department chair at Bucks County Technical High School. Elected Commissioner in 2019, named board chair in 2022, and re-elected in 2023, he won the Democratic primary roughly 65%–35% and sits on the DCCC's "Red to Blue" list. His campaign centers on economic fairness and kitchen-table issues. He faces Republican incumbent Brian Fitzpatrick, a former FBI agent first elected in 2016.
