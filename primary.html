Executive Summary

With the May 19 Democratic primary just over a month away, attention is concentrating on the PA-01 showdown between Bucks County Commissioner Bob Harvie and progressive challenger Lucia Simonelli, plus a crowded slate of Democratic challengers attempting to flip state Senate and state House seats. The League of Women Voters of Bucks County has announced a PA-01 candidate forum on April 27 at Doylestown United Methodist Church, and Democrat Kristin Egan (HD-142) made news this month becoming the first state candidate to formally oppose the $227M PennDOT US-1 reconstruction project. National Democrats continue to target PA-01 and the 16th state Senate district as top flip opportunities.

U.S. Congress — 1st Congressional District

Bob Harvie

PA-01 · Falls Township
Bucks County Commissioner who announced his congressional bid in April 2025. A lifelong Bucks County resident born in Bristol Borough, Harvie graduated magna cum laude from George Washington University and spent 20 years as a teacher and Social Studies Department chair at Bucks County Technical High School. Elected County Commissioner in 2019 and named board chair in 2022, he was re-elected in 2023. His campaign centers on economic fairness and kitchen-table issues.
WHYY — "Meet Bob Harvie and Lucia Simonelli, the Democrats fighting to unseat Brian Fitzpatrick in Congress" (April 2026)
WHYY's 2026 primary voter guide profiles the two Democrats competing to take on Republican incumbent Brian Fitzpatrick in November, framing Harvie as the establishment-endorsed front-runner touting his cross-aisle record as a commissioner in a county Trump narrowly carried in 2024.
Philadelphia Inquirer — "Meet Bob Harvie, Democrats' best chance at defeating Brian Fitzpatrick and helping win back the House" (March 29, 2026)
The Inquirer's profile elevates Harvie as national Democrats' top pick to flip PA-01, highlighting the Bucks County Democratic Committee endorsement and support from Lt. Gov. Austin Davis, Rep. Madeleine Dean, Rep. Chris Deluzio, and DA Joe Khan.
City & State Pennsylvania — "Stelson, Cognetti and Perry are among PA's top congressional fundraisers in 2026" (April 2026)
First-quarter FEC reports show Harvie in a strong financial position relative to other Democratic House challengers across Pennsylvania, with national Democratic groups investing in the PA-01 flip.
League of Women Voters of Bucks County — "Candidate Forum: Democratic Primary for PA's 1st Congressional District" (April 2026)
The LWV announced it will host a forum with Harvie and Simonelli on Monday, April 27, 7–9 p.m. at Doylestown United Methodist Church. A livestream and recording will be available.

Lucia Simonelli

PA-01 · Quakertown Borough
Holds a PhD in mathematics and has served as a scientific advisor in the U.S. Senate on energy and climate policy. Born in Lansdale and now settled in Quakertown, she directs programs for a nonprofit focused on scaling clean-air and carbon-removal technologies. She is running as a progressive, zeroing in on universal healthcare and Medicare for All.
Bucks County Beacon — "Could Progressive Congressional Candidate Lucia Simonelli Shock PA-01 With a Victory in the May Democratic Primary?" (February 2026)
A long-form interview positioning Simonelli as the progressive alternative to Harvie, emphasizing universal healthcare, climate policy, and her grassroots organizing work with Upper Bucks United Indivisible.
WHYY — "Fitzpatrick challengers are courting Bucks voters" (April 2026)
Covers Simonelli's appearances at recent PA-01 town halls, where she has drawn sharp contrasts with Harvie on Medicare for All and corporate influence in Democratic primaries.
Bucks County Herald — "Democratic primary in PA-01 down to two candidates" (March 2026)
After the March 10 ballot deadline, the Democratic field narrowed to a two-way Harvie-vs-Simonelli race; the Herald notes Simonelli is making affordability and healthcare the core of her closing message.

Pennsylvania State Senate

Steve Santarsiero — Incumbent

10th Senatorial District
Has represented the 10th District since 2019 and serves as chair of the Bucks County Democrats and as Minority Caucus Secretary in the State Senate. Running unopposed in the May primary. Active on minimum wage, PECO rate increases, and K-12 cellphone policy.
Bucks County Beacon — "State Senator Steve Santarsiero: We Are Bucks County Democrats, Big D and Little D" (February 2026)
In a guest column, Santarsiero lays out the Bucks Democratic Committee's strategy for 2026, emphasizing a coordinated slate from Congress down to state House and flipping Republican-held seats.
Pennsylvania Senate Democrats — "Santarsiero statement on PA House passage of a $15 minimum wage" (April 2026)
Santarsiero praised the House vote as long overdue and urged the Senate to act, framing the minimum wage as a central kitchen-table issue heading into November.

Eileen Hartnett Albillar

6th Senatorial District
Bucks County Clerk of Courts who launched her bid in January 2026 to unseat Republican Frank Farry. Former Warrington Township supervisor with a 25-year background in social work and nonprofit leadership. Endorsed by Senate Democratic Leader Jay Costa, Sen. Santarsiero, and Commissioners Harvie and Ellis-Marseglia. Campaign focuses on affordability, SEPTA funding, and working families.
Bucks County Beacon — "Long-Time Bucks County Public Servant Eileen Hartnett Albillar Wants to Help Democrats Flip Pennsylvania's State Senate" (January 2026)
Profile of Hartnett Albillar's launch, walking through her social-work background, Warrington supervisor tenure, and her argument that Farry is out of step with a rapidly diversifying Lower Bucks.
LevittownNow.com — "Democratic County Row Officer Launches Bid For State Senate" (January 21, 2026)
LevittownNow covered the rollout announcement, highlighting the unified Bucks Democratic slate backing her and her focus on affordability and SEPTA funding as the top SD-6 issues.

Mark Pinsley vs. Bradley Merkl-Gump — Contested Primary

16th Senatorial District
A key contested Democratic primary and one of the most-watched state Senate races of 2026. Winner faces Republican incumbent Jarrett Coleman in November — a seat Democrats see as critical to flipping the state Senate.
Bucks County Herald — "Contested Democratic primary set in Pennsylvania's 16th Senatorial District" (March 2026)
After Wayne Codner withdrew, the SD-16 primary is a two-way race between Lehigh County Controller Mark Pinsley — on his third bid for the seat — and Pennridge School Board member Bradley Merkl-Gump, an 8th-grade social-studies teacher from Perkasie.
Spotlight PA — "The 2026 primaries likely to shape the Pennsylvania House and Senate" (March 2026)
Spotlight PA names SD-16 among the most consequential 2026 primaries statewide, noting the race's ideological contrast and its outsized role in determining which party controls the chamber.
Armchair Lehigh Valley — "Two Democrats left in 16th Senate District's primary" (Spring 2026)
Tracks the field narrowing and compares the two candidates' fundraising, endorsements, and theories of how to beat Coleman in a swingy district.

Pennsylvania State House

Bryan Allen

18th District · Bensalem
Former Bensalem Township council member and chief of staff for State Rep. Tina Davis, 47. Introduced the first casino tax rebate for Bensalem residents and serves as vice chair of the Bucks County Water and Sewer Authority Board. Challenging Republican incumbent K.C. Tomlinson.
Bucks County Beacon — "Bensalem's Bryan Allen Wants to Help Democrats Expand Their Majority in the Pennsylvania State House" (February 2026)
In-depth profile of Allen's platform — public education funding, property-tax relief via increased state support, healthcare access, and working-family economics — and his read on why HD-18 is winnable.
Bensalem Weekly — "Meet Bryan Allen: Candidate for Bensalem's 18th District State Representative" (February 2026)
A local Q&A covering Allen's Conwell-Egan and Bloomsburg background, his legislative-staff experience, and the casino tax rebate he championed for Bensalem residents.
LevittownNow.com — "Longtime Legislative Staffer Announces Campaign For State Representative" (February 2, 2026)
Covers Allen's formal launch and quotes Rep. Tina Davis, his longtime boss, endorsing his candidacy.

Jim Prokopiak — Incumbent

140th District · Falls Township
Won the seat in a February 2024 special election and re-elected in November 2024. Former Falls Township supervisor (2002–2009) and Temple University law graduate who practices law representing injured railroad workers. Sole Democrat on the primary ballot.

Tina Davis — Incumbent

141st District · Bristol Township
Has represented Bristol Township and Bristol Borough since 2010 and serves as House Majority Caucus Secretary. Priorities include economic development, pay equity, and property tax reform.
Bucks County Herald — "Nominating petitions show no contested Pa. House primaries in Bucks County" (March 2026)
The Herald's round-up confirms Davis is unopposed in the primary and will face Republican challenger Kenneth James Velez of Bristol Township in November.

Kristin Egan

142nd District · Langhorne
Neshaminy School Board director and 20-year public school teacher challenging Republican incumbent Joe Hogan. Former Title I Coordinator, union officer, and chief negotiator. Platform: education funding reform, public safety, and cost of living.
Bucks County Beacon — "Democratic State House Candidate Kristin Egan Goes on Record to Oppose Controversial US 1 Project in Lower Bucks County" (April 2026)
Egan became the first state-level candidate to publicly oppose PennDOT's $227M US-1 reconstruction plan, citing impacts on local communities and saying infrastructure dollars should prioritize safer local roads and transit.
Bucks County Beacon — "Democrat Kristin Egan Announces Bid to Unseat Bucks County Incumbent Republican Joe Hogan to Represent HD-142" (February 2026)
Launch profile detailing Egan's 20-year classroom career, school-board tenure, and her theory that a public-education-focused challenger can take back a seat Hogan won narrowly in 2022.
LevittownNow.com — "Neshaminy School Board Member Enters Race For State Representative" (February 12, 2026)
Covers Egan's announcement and her pitch to Lower Bucks voters on education funding, property taxes, and public safety.

Tim Hayes

143rd District · Dublin
Dublin Borough Council president, Army National Guard veteran, and environmental policy expert running against Republican incumbent Shelby Labs. Elected to council in 2019 at age 21 — the youngest elected official in Bucks County at the time — and elected council president by his peers in 2023. Works as state policy manager for an environmental advocacy group. Priorities include alternative energy, regulating AI data farms, affordability, and education.
Bucks County Beacon — "Dublin Borough Council President Tim Hayes Announces Run for Pennsylvania State Representative" (December 2025)
Launch story detailing Hayes's environmental-policy background and his unusual platform plank on regulating resource-heavy AI data-center development in Upper Bucks.
Bucks County Herald — "Nominating petitions show no contested Pa. House primaries in Bucks County" (March 2026)
Confirms Hayes is the sole Democrat on the ballot in HD-143 and will face two-term Republican Shelby Labs in November.

Brian Munroe — Incumbent

144th District · Warminster
Former Radnor Township police officer and Warminster Township supervisor. Unseated Republican Todd Polinchock in 2022 by 515 votes and re-elected in 2024 by 1,065 votes. Endorsed by Conservation Voters of PA. Represents a classic swing district.
Conservation Voters of Pennsylvania — "Brian Munroe — HD-144 endorsement" (2026)
CVPA re-endorsed Munroe, citing his votes on clean-energy incentives and environmental protection and noting HD-144 remains one of the most contested seats in Southeast PA.

Les Mavus

145th District · Nockamixon
Nockamixon Township resident and inspector of elections challenging Republican incumbent Craig Staats in a traditionally red Upper Bucks district.
Bucks County Herald — "Nominating petitions show no contested Pa. House primaries in Bucks County" (March 2026)
Confirms Mavus is on the ballot as the Democratic nominee in HD-145, setting up a November contest against Staats in a district that includes Quakertown, Riegelsville, and much of Upper Bucks.

Monica Weninger

178th District · Warwick
25-year public school science teacher and Science Department Chair at Wissahickon School District. Auditor in Warwick Township — the first Democrat elected to that office in decades — and a past president of the League of Women Voters of Bucks County. Challenging Republican incumbent Kristin Marcell.
Bucks Independence — "Several Democrats declare candidacies against GOP incumbent state lawmakers" (February 10, 2026)
Round-up of 2026 Democratic challengers profiles Weninger's background as a veteran science teacher and LWV leader, and her pitch that Marcell is too conservative for a district that has been trending blue.
