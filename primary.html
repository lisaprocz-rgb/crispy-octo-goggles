Executive summary. The May 19 Democratic primary is over and the November matchups are set. In PA-01, Bucks County Commissioner Bob Harvie won the congressional nomination with roughly 65% over Lucia Simonelli and will challenge Republican Rep. Brian Fitzpatrick — a race the DCCC has added to its "Red to Blue" program. The only contested legislative primary in the area, the 16th State Senate seat, went to Mark Pinsley over Bradley Merkl-Gump. Every Bucks County Democratic State House and remaining Senate candidate ran unopposed and has advanced to the general, with Democrats framing Bucks as the hinge for flipping both the U.S. House and the long-GOP state Senate.

Bob Harvie — PA-01 (Falls Township) Bucks County Commissioner who announced his congressional bid in April 2025.
