Executive Summary

With 20 days left until the May 19 primary, the PA-01 Democratic congressional race dominates today's coverage following Monday night's League of Women Voters forum in Middletown between Bob Harvie and Lucia Simonelli — recapped this morning in The Inquirer, WHYY, LevittownNow, NewtownPANow, North Penn Now and Central Bucks News. A separate Inquirer story today reports a fresh skirmish between Harvie and Rep. Brian Fitzpatrick over open primaries. In the 16th Senate District, Mark Pinsley and Bradley Merkl-Gump met for their primary debate at Univest Public Media Center in Bethlehem on April 28.

U.S. Congress — 1st Congressional District

Bob Harvie — Falls Township

Bucks County Commissioner who announced his congressional bid in April 2025. Lifelong Bucks County resident born in Bristol Borough, magna cum laude graduate of George Washington University, spent 20 years as a teacher and Social Studies Department chair at Bucks County Technical High School. Elected County Commissioner in 2019, named board chair in 2022, re-elected in 2023. Campaign centers on economic fairness and kitchen-table issues.

Lucia Simonelli — Quakertown Borough

Holds a PhD in mathematics and has served as a scientific advisor in the U.S. Senate. Born in Lansdale, now settled in Quakertown. Directs programs for a nonprofit focused on scaling clean-air technologies. Running as a progressive, zeroing in on universal healthcare and Medicare for All.

Pennsylvania State Senate

Steve Santarsiero — 10th Senatorial District (Incumbent)

Has represented the 10th District since 2019 and serves as chair of the Bucks County Democrats. Running unopposed in the May primary. Active on minimum wage, PECO rate increases, and other legislative issues.

PA Senate Democrats — State Senator Steve Santarsiero Statement on PECO's Proposed Rate Increase (April 2026)
Santarsiero called PECO's proposed hike "out of touch" with Bucks County's cost-of-living crisis and pushed HB1834 plus the Governor's GRID Plan to make data-center developers pay for the energy infrastructure they require.
WHYY — PECO withdraws rate hike proposal under pressure from Gov. Shapiro (April 2026)
PECO pulled its rate-increase request after Gov. Shapiro and a Bucks-area legislative coalition that included Santarsiero publicly opposed it; the utility may refile a 12.5% hike for 2027.
Bucks County Herald — Bucks County lawmakers oppose PECO's rate hikes request (April 2026)
Local coverage of Santarsiero and other Lower Bucks lawmakers formally opposing PECO's rate-hike filing before the PUC.

Eileen Hartnett Albillar — 6th Senatorial District

Bucks County Clerk of Courts who launched her bid in January 2026 to unseat Republican Frank Farry. Former Warrington Township supervisor with a background in social work and nonprofit leadership. Endorsed by Senate Democratic Leader Jay Costa, Sen. Santarsiero, Commissioners Harvie and Ellis-Marseglia. Focuses on affordability, SEPTA funding, and working families.

The Zekely Podcast — Episode 68 — Pennsylvania State Senate Candidate Eileen Hartnett Albillar (April 19, 2026)
A long-form interview in which Hartnett Albillar lays out her positions on healthcare, education and gun safety and frames SD-6 as a key seat in flipping the Pennsylvania Senate.

16th Senatorial District — Mark Pinsley vs. Bradley Merkl-Gump

Contested Democratic primary between Lehigh County Controller Mark Pinsley and Pennridge School Board member Bradley Merkl-Gump (Perkasie, eighth-grade social-studies teacher). Winner will face Republican Sen. Jarrett Coleman in November in a key race for flipping the state Senate.

LehighValleyNews — Pinsley, Merkl-Gump to go head-to-head in 16th state Senate District's Democratic primary debate (April 28, 2026)
Lehigh Valley Public Media hosted the only 16th-district primary debate at Univest Public Media Center in Bethlehem, with the two Democrats sparring over experience, electability and approach to flipping a Coleman-held seat.
Bucks County Herald — Two Democrats remain in race to oppose state Sen. Jarrett Coleman (April 2026)
A Herald look at the now two-way contest in SD-16 after the field winnowed, with Pinsley pitching elected-office experience and Merkl-Gump pitching grassroots educator credibility.

Neshaminy School Board director and 20-year public school teacher challenging Republican Joe Hogan. First state candidate to publicly oppose the $227M PennDOT US-1 reconstruction project. Platform: education funding reform, public safety, cost of living.

Dublin Borough Council president, Army National Guard veteran, and environmental policy expert running against Republican Shelby Labs. Priorities include alternative energy, regulating AI data farms, affordability and education.
