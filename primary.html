Executive Summary — With 28 days until the May 19 primary, the PA-01 congressional race is heating up: Bob Harvie and Lucia Simonelli are preparing for a League of Women Voters debate on April 27 in Doylestown. Outside-money concerns are dominating the broader Democratic primary conversation, and down-ballot state House Democrats — particularly Kristin Egan (HD-142) and Tim Hayes (HD-143) — continue pressing affordability and infrastructure themes. Most Bucks County state House primaries are uncontested, with action shifting to November's general election targets.
U.S. Congress — 1st Congressional District
Bob Harvie — Falls Township
Bucks County Commissioner who announced his congressional bid in April 2025. Lifelong Bucks County resident born in Bristol Borough, graduated magna cum laude from George Washington University, spent 20 years as a teacher and Social Studies Department chair at Bucks County Technical High School. Elected County Commissioner in 2019, named board chair in 2022, re-elected in 2023. Campaign centers on economic fairness and kitchen-table issues.

Voter-guide profile walks through Harvie's teaching background, Falls Township planning-commission roots, and his argument that his executive record as commissioner positions him best to flip PA-01. Frames the May 19 primary as a two-person race between Harvie and Simonelli.
PoliticsPA — "Can Bob Harvie End Brian Fitzpatrick's Tenure in Congress?" (April 2026)
Analysis piece weighing Harvie's countywide name recognition, fundraising pace, and endorsements from Patrick Murphy and Peter Kostmayer against Fitzpatrick's brand-as-moderate durability. Concludes PA-01 is one of the DCCC's top national targets.

Lucia Simonelli — Quakertown Borough
Holds a PhD in mathematics and has served as a scientific advisor in the U.S. Senate. Born in Lansdale, now settled in Quakertown. Directs programs for a nonprofit focused on scaling clean-air technologies. Running as a progressive, zeroing in on universal healthcare and Medicare for All.

Long-form interview covering Simonelli's path from academic mathematician to U.S. Senate science adviser, her Medicare for All platform, and her argument that a progressive can outperform expectations in the crowded suburban district.

Recap of a PA-01 candidate town hall where Simonelli pressed climate and health-care contrasts with Harvie. Notes a League of Women Voters debate scheduled for 7 p.m. April 27 at Doylestown United Methodist Church.

Endorsed by Senate Democratic Leader Jay Costa, Sen. Santarsiero, Commissioners Harvie and Ellis-Marseglia. Focuses on affordability, SEPTA funding, and working families.
