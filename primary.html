U.S. Congress
Bob Harvie (D) vs. Rep. Brian Fitzpatrick (R, incumbent) — 1st Congressional District Bucks County Commissioner who announced his congressional bid in April 2025. A lifelong Bucks County resident born in Bristol Borough, he graduated magna cum laude from George Washington University and spent 20 years as a teacher and Social Studies Department chair at Bucks County Technical High School. Elected County Commissioner in 2019, named board chair in 2022 and re-elected in 2023. He won the Democratic primary roughly 65%–35% and sits on the DCCC's "Red to Blue" list, running on economic fairness and kitchen-table issues against incumbent Republican Brian Fitzpatrick of Falls Township.
