Executive Summary: Today is primary election day in Pennsylvania — polls are open 7 a.m. to 8 p.m. The marquee Bucks County race is the PA-01 Democratic congressional primary between Bob Harvie and Lucia Simonelli, with Gov. Shapiro's late endorsement and a DCCC "Red to Blue" designation boosting Harvie heading into the count. Down-ballot, the only other contested Democratic primary touching Bucks is the 16th State Senate race between Mark Pinsley and Bradley Merkl-Gump; all other Bucks Democratic legislative candidates are unopposed today and pivot directly to November. U.S. Congress — 1st District
Bob Harvie — PA-01 (Falls Township) Bucks County Commissioner who announced his congressional bid in April 2025. Lifelong Bucks County resident born in Bristol Borough, magna cum laude graduate of George Washington University, spent 20 years as a teacher and Social Studies Department chair at Bucks County Technical High School. Elected County Commissioner in 2019, named board chair in 2022, re-elected in 2023. Campaign centers on economic fairness and kitchen-table issues.

Pennsylvania State Senate
Steve Santarsiero — 10th District (Incumbent) Has represented the 10th District since 2019 and serves as chair of the Bucks County Democrats and Minority Caucus Secretary. Running unopposed in today's primary. Previously represented the 31st PA House district 2009–2016. Active on minimum wage, PECO rate increases, and other legislative issues. No new individual coverage in the last 48 hours; Santarsiero is unopposed today and continues general-election preparations.
Eileen Hartnett Albillar — 6th District Bucks County Clerk of Courts who launched her bid in January 2026 to unseat Republican Frank Farry. Former Warrington Township supervisor with 25+ years in social work and nonprofit leadership. Endorsed by Senate Democratic Leader Jay Costa, Sen.
