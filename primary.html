Executive Summary

With 30 days until the May 19 primary, the NRCC escalated attacks on congressional frontrunner Bob Harvie this week while both Democratic candidates prepare for the League of Women Voters debate on April 27 at Doylestown United Methodist Church. Most state House incumbents are running unopposed in the primary, setting the stage for key general election matchups in swing districts — particularly Bryan Allen's challenge in the 18th and Kristin Egan's bid in the 142nd. State Senate races in the 6th and 16th districts remain top targets for Democrats hoping to flip the chamber.

U.S. Congress — 1st Congressional District

Democratic Primary · Challenging Rep. Brian Fitzpatrick (R)

Bob Harvie — Falls Township

Bucks County Commissioner who announced his congressional bid in April 2025. Lifelong Bucks County resident born in Bristol Borough, graduated magna cum laude from George Washington University, spent 20 years as a teacher and Social Studies Department chair at Bucks County Technical High School. Elected County Commissioner in 2019, named board chair in 2022, re-elected in 2023. Campaign centers on economic fairness and kitchen-table issues.

NRCC — "This Tax Day, Remember: Corrupt Bob Harvie Slashed Firefighters' Tax Benefit" (Apr 15, 2026)

The National Republican Congressional Committee amplified Tax Day attacks accusing Harvie of rolling back a local tax benefit for volunteer firefighters during his time as commissioner — part of a coordinated opposition research push ahead of the primary.

NRCC — "REMINDER: Corrupt Bob Harvie Made Tax Day Worse" (Apr 15, 2026)

Second NRCC release the same day reiterating that Harvie presided over a nearly 10% county tax increase; committee frames the hit as a preview of general-election messaging if Harvie wins the Democratic nomination.

NRCC — "Corrupt Harvie Shamed into Condemning Sexual Predator" (Apr 13, 2026)

Follow-up to an April 12 release targeting Harvie's initial silence on allegations against fellow Democrat Rep. Eric Swalwell; NRCC claims Harvie only spoke up under political pressure.

Philadelphia Inquirer — "Meet Bob Harvie, Democrats' best chance at defeating Brian Fitzpatrick and helping win back the House" (Mar 29, 2026)

Profile positioning Harvie as the establishment frontrunner in a district national Democrats see as pivotal for flipping the U.S. House; lays out his commissioner record and Bristol Borough roots.

Lucia Simonelli — Quakertown Borough

Holds a PhD in mathematics and has served as a scientific advisor in the U.S. Senate. Born in Lansdale, now settled in Quakertown. Directs programs for a nonprofit focused on scaling clean-air technologies. Running as a progressive, zeroing in on universal healthcare and Medicare for All.

WHYY — "Pa. election 2026: Meet Bob Harvie and Lucia Simonelli, Dems fighting to unseat Brian Fitzpatrick" (April 2026)

WHYY's primary voter guide contrasts Simonelli's progressive, Medicare-for-All platform with Harvie's establishment-backed moderate pitch and details her path from academic mathematician to U.S. Senate energy and climate adviser.

Doylestown Patch — "Bucks Congressional Candidate Hosting Discussion On Affordability Crisis" (April 2026)

Simonelli announced a community conversation on the affordability crisis as she leans into kitchen-table economics heading into the final stretch before the May 19 primary.

Bucks County Beacon — "Could Progressive Congressional Candidate Lucia Simonelli Shock PA-01 With a Victory in the May Democratic Primary?" (Feb 2026, ongoing coverage)

In-depth interview with Simonelli on her universal healthcare push and the progressive case against Harvie; continues to drive the narrative of a two-lane primary.

📅 Upcoming: League of Women Voters of Bucks County will host a Harvie–Simonelli debate on Monday, April 27, 7–9 p.m. at Doylestown United Methodist Church.

Pennsylvania State Senate

Key races to flip the chamber

Steve Santarsiero — 10th Senatorial District (Incumbent)

Has represented the 10th District since 2019 and serves as chair of the Bucks County Democrats. Running unopposed in the May primary. Active on minimum wage, PECO rate increases, and other legislative issues.

Bucks County Beacon — "Raising Pennsylvania's $7.25 Minimum Wage Is About Economic Justice and Improving Economic Competitiveness" (Mar 2026, continuing coverage)

Santarsiero op-ed pressing Senate Republican leadership to bring the House-passed $15 minimum wage bill up for a vote, calling the state's $7.25 floor a competitive liability against every neighboring state.

Bucks County Beacon — "Three Bucks County Republican State Lawmakers Voted to Keep Pennsylvania's Minimum Wage at $7.25 Per Hour" (Mar 2026)

Reporting that circulated this week highlights Santarsiero's role framing the minimum wage vote as a contrast issue for Bucks County Democratic challengers in the fall.

Eileen Hartnett Albillar — 6th Senatorial District

Bucks County Clerk of Courts who launched her bid in January 2026 to unseat Republican Frank Farry. Former Warrington Township supervisor with a background in social work and nonprofit leadership. Endorsed by Senate Democratic Leader Jay Costa, Sen. Santarsiero, Commissioners Harvie and Ellis-Marseglia. Focuses on affordability, SEPTA funding, and working families.

Bucks County Beacon — "Long-Time Bucks County Public Servant Eileen Hartnett Albillar Wants to Help Democrats Flip Pennsylvania's State Senate" (Jan 2026, ongoing)

Profile framing Hartnett Albillar's 25-year social work career as the backbone of a campaign centered on affordability, transit funding, and pro-choice priorities against incumbent Republican Sen. Frank Farry.

LevittownNow.com — "Democratic County Row Officer Launches Bid For State Senate" (Jan 21, 2026)

Launch coverage details endorsements from Jay Costa, Steve Santarsiero, Commissioners Harvie and Ellis-Marseglia, and Represent PA — positioning her as the party-consensus candidate in a must-flip district.

Mark Pinsley vs. Bradley Merkl-Gump — 16th Senatorial District

Contested Democratic primary for a Lehigh/Bucks district seen as a key flip opportunity. Mark Pinsley is the Lehigh County Controller making his third bid for the seat. Bradley Merkl-Gump is a Pennridge School Board director and middle-school social studies teacher from Perkasie.

Bucks County Herald — "Contested Democratic primary set in Pennsylvania's 16th Senatorial District" (2026, ongoing coverage)

After Codner withdrew, the primary has narrowed to Pinsley and Merkl-Gump with the winner facing Republican Sen. Jarrett Coleman in November.

Spotlight PA — "Pennsylvania legislative primaries to watch in 2026" (Mar 2026)

Spotlight PA flags the 16th District race as one of the most consequential legislative primaries in the state — and potentially decisive for control of the Pennsylvania Senate.

The Brown and White — "Mark Pinsley drops out of PA-07 race, shifts focus to state senate" (Feb 26, 2026)

Pinsley ended his congressional bid to concentrate on the 16th Senate race, citing campaign-finance realities; his eighth campaign in ten years and his third run at this seat.

Pennsylvania State House

Bucks County Democratic candidates — most unopposed in primary

Bryan Allen — 18th District (Bensalem)

Former Bensalem Township council member and chief of staff for State Rep. Tina Davis, 47. Introduced the first casino tax rebate for Bensalem residents. Vice chair of Bucks County Water and Sewer Authority Board. Challenging Republican K.C. Tomlinson.

Bucks County Beacon — "Bensalem's Bryan Allen Wants to Help Democrats Expand Their Majority in the Pennsylvania State House" (Feb 2026, continuing)

In-depth Q&A on Allen's plans to bring his local council experience — including the Bensalem casino tax rebate — to Harrisburg, and how he's framing Rep. Tomlinson as out of step with a district that has trended bluer.

Bensalem Patch — "Ex-Councilman Seeks To Unseat State Rep. Tomlinson" (Feb 2026)

Launch profile outlining Allen's pitch of Bensalem roots and legislative-staff experience under Tina Davis, and Tomlinson's February reelection announcement set the general-election matchup.

Tim Brennan — 29th District (Doylestown), Incumbent

Attorney and former Doylestown Borough councilman representing the 29th District since 2023. Unopposed in the Democratic primary.

No new coverage in the last 48 hours.

Perry Warren — 31st District (Newtown), Incumbent

Has represented the 31st District (Lower Makefield, Newtown Borough, Newtown Township, Upper Makefield, Yardley) since 2017. Unopposed in the primary.

No new coverage in the last 48 hours.

Jim Prokopiak — 140th District (Falls Township), Incumbent

Won seat in February 2024 special election. Former Falls Township supervisor and Temple University law graduate. Sole Democrat on primary ballot.

No new coverage in the last 48 hours. Prokopiak continues his focus on worker-injury and transportation issues from his legal background.

Tina Davis — 141st District (Bristol Township), Incumbent

Has represented Bristol Township and Bristol Borough since 2010. Priorities include economic development, pay equity, property tax reform.

No new coverage in the last 48 hours. Davis faces Republican Kenneth James Velez in the November general election.

Kristin Egan — 142nd District (Langhorne)

Neshaminy School Board director and 20-year public school teacher challenging Republican Joe Hogan. First state candidate to publicly oppose $227M PennDOT US-1 reconstruction project. Platform: education funding reform, public safety, cost of living.

Bucks County Beacon — "Democratic State House Candidate Kristin Egan Goes on Record to Oppose Controversial US 1 Project in Lower Bucks County" (April 2026)

Egan becomes the first state-office candidate to formally oppose PennDOT's roughly $227 million US-1 RC3 reconstruction, calling it "one of the most significant issues" of her race against incumbent Rep. Joe Hogan.

LevittownNow.com — "Neshaminy School Board Member Enters Race For State Representative" (Feb 12, 2026)

Campaign launch coverage highlights Egan's 20 years in the classroom and her pitch to voters in a district Hogan flipped in 2022 by a slim margin and held by a wider one in 2024.

Tim Hayes — 143rd District (Dublin)

Dublin Borough Council president, Army National Guard veteran, environmental policy expert running against Republican Shelby Labs. Priorities include alternative energy, regulating AI data farms, affordability, education.

Bucks County Beacon — "Dublin Borough Council President Tim Hayes Announces Run for Pennsylvania State Representative" (Dec 2025, continuing)

Hayes is running on regulating AI data centers before they "starve communities" of water and power, and backs House Bill 501 (Alternative Energy Portfolio Standards Act) to shift the burden onto large energy end-users.

Brian Munroe — 144th District (Warminster), Incumbent

Former Radnor Township police officer and Warminster Township supervisor. Unseated Republican Todd Polinchock in 2022 by 515 votes, re-elected in 2024 by 1,065 votes. Endorsed by Conservation Voters of PA. Swing district.

No new coverage in the last 48 hours. In the general election, Munroe faces Republican Michael J. Murphy of Warrington.

Les Mavus — 145th District (Nockamixon)

Nockamixon Township resident and inspector of elections challenging Republican Craig Staats.

No new coverage in the last 48 hours. Mavus is the Democratic nominee for the general election against incumbent Rep. Craig Staats in a district seen as heavily Republican.

Monica Weninger — 178th District (Warwick)

25-year public school science teacher and Science Department Chair at Wissahickon School District. Auditor in Warwick Township — first Democrat elected to that office in decades. Challenging Republican Kristin Marcell.

Bucks Independence — "Several Democrats declare candidacies against GOP incumbent state lawmakers" (Feb 10, 2026)

Round-up places Weninger's candidacy alongside Egan, Allen, and Hayes as part of a coordinated Democratic push to unseat Republican incumbents in Bucks County.
