Executive Summary: With 25 days until the May 19 primary, the marquee story today is the Q1 fundraising filing showing GOP incumbent Brian Fitzpatrick holds a $7M cash-on-hand advantage over both PA-01 Democratic challengers. Bob Harvie outraised Lucia Simonelli roughly 17-to-1 ($430K vs. $25K) heading into the final stretch, and the two square off Monday night at the League of Women Voters forum in Doylestown. Down-ballot, Bucks County's nine state House Democrats appear largely set after no contested primaries materialized; the contested 16th Senate primary between Mark Pinsley and Bradley Merkl-Gump remains the key flip target.

U.S. Congress — 1st Congressional District

Bob Harvie — Falls Township

Bucks County Commissioner who announced his congressional bid in April 2025. Lifelong Bucks County resident born in Bristol Borough, graduated magna cum laude from George Washington University, spent 20 years as a teacher and Social Studies Department chair at Bucks County Technical High School. Elected County Commissioner in 2019, named board chair in 2022, re-elected in 2023. Campaign centers on economic fairness and kitchen-table issues.

Central Bucks News — "Fitzpatrick has $7M more on hand than Democrats challenging him" (Apr 23, 2026)

Q1 FEC filings show Rep. Brian Fitzpatrick entered April with $7.65M cash on hand, dwarfing Harvie's $430K raised between January and March. Despite the gap, national Democrats continue to view PA-01 as one of their strongest 2026 flip opportunities.

WHYY — "PA election 2026: 1st Congressional District primary voter guide" (Apr 2026)

Side-by-side guide profiling Harvie and Simonelli as the two Democrats on the May 19 ballot. Frames Harvie as the establishment-endorsed candidate with backing from the Bucks and Montgomery County Democratic Committees and a deep bench of local electeds.

Bucks County Herald — "National Democrats target PA-01 as flippable" (Apr 2026)

DCCC adds PA-01 to its 2026 priority target list, citing Bucks County's purple electorate and Harvie's countywide name ID from two commissioner wins. Article notes the seat is among a small handful nationally that could determine House control.

Lucia Simonelli — Quakertown Borough

Holds a PhD in mathematics and has served as a scientific advisor in the U.S. Senate. Born in Lansdale, now settled in Quakertown. Directs programs for a nonprofit focused on scaling clean-air technologies. Running as a progressive, zeroing in on universal healthcare and Medicare for All.

Bucks County Beacon — "Lucia Simonelli Wants PA-01 Democrats to Vote Their Hopes, Not Their Fears" (Apr 2026)

Profile makes the closing argument for Simonelli's grassroots, anti-establishment campaign, contrasting her Medicare for All platform with what she describes as Harvie's safer, kitchen-table messaging. Article highlights endorsements from Indivisible Greater Jenkintown, Indivisible Lower Merion, and Track AIPAC.

Central Bucks News — "Fitzpatrick has $7M more on hand than Democrats challenging him" (Apr 23, 2026)

Same Q1 filing report shows Simonelli raised just under $25K from January through March — about 1/17 of Harvie's total — underscoring the resource gap inside the Democratic primary as well as against Fitzpatrick.
