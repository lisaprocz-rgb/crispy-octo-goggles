Executive Summary — Pennsylvania's May 19 primary is in the books, and the Bucks County matchups for November are now set. In PA-01, Bucks County Commissioner Bob Harvie cruised to the Democratic nomination with 65% over Lucia Simonelli and will face five-term Republican Brian Fitzpatrick — who drew a pointed public warning from President Trump on the campaign's opening day. Mark Pinsley won the contested 16th state Senate Democratic primary over Bradley Merkl-Gump and advances to challenge GOP Sen. Jarrett Coleman. With no contested Bucks County state House primaries, every local Democrat advanced unopposed, locking in their general-election opponents. Bob Harvie — PA-01 (Falls Township) Bucks County Commissioner who announced his congressional bid in April 2025.
