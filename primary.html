Executive Summary

With just under four weeks until the May 19 primary, the PA-01 congressional race remains the marquee contest as Bob Harvie and Lucia Simonelli sharpen contrasts ahead of a League of Women Voters candidate forum set for April 27 in Doylestown. In state legislative news, HD-142 challenger Kristin Egan drew fresh coverage this month after becoming the first state candidate to publicly oppose the $227M PennDOT US-1 reconstruction project, and Bucks County Democrats continue pressing on PECO rate hikes and a stalled minimum wage increase as signature kitchen-table issues.

U.S. Congress — 1st Congressional District

Bob Harvie · Falls Township · PA-01

Bucks County Commissioner who announced his congressional bid in April 2025. Lifelong Bucks County resident born in Bristol Borough, graduated magna cum laude from George Washington University, spent 20 years as a teacher and Social Studies Department chair at Bucks County Technical High School. Elected County Commissioner in 2019, named board chair in 2022, re-elected in 2023. Campaign centers on economic fairness and kitchen-table issues.

WHYY — "Meet Bob Harvie and Lucia Simonelli, the Democrats fighting to unseat Brian Fitzpatrick in Congress" (April 2026)

Side-by-side voter guide profiling both PA-01 Democrats ahead of the May 19 primary. Notes that Harvie launched his campaign a year ago with a broad slate of Democratic establishment endorsements and is widely seen as the front-runner.

Philadelphia Inquirer — "Meet Bob Harvie, Democrats' best chance at defeating Brian Fitzpatrick and helping win back the House" (March 29, 2026)

Feature on Harvie's background as a former social studies teacher and county commissioner, and on national Democratic strategists' view of PA-01 as one of the most winnable Republican-held seats in the country.

Bucks County Herald — "National Democrats target PA-01 as flippable" (April 2026)

Reports the DCCC has added PA-01 to its 44-district "Districts in Play" list, naming incumbent Brian Fitzpatrick a top 2026 target and elevating national attention on the Harvie–Simonelli primary.

Lucia Simonelli · Quakertown Borough · PA-01

Holds a PhD in mathematics and has served as a scientific advisor in the U.S. Senate. Born in Lansdale, now settled in Quakertown. Directs programs for a nonprofit focused on scaling clean-air technologies. Running as a progressive, zeroing in on universal healthcare and Medicare for All.

Bucks County Beacon — "Lucia Simonelli Wants PA-01 Democrats to Vote Their Hopes, Not Their Fears" (April 2026)

Campaign-trail profile ahead of the primary, in which Simonelli leans into a progressive platform — universal healthcare, affordability, climate action — and argues a bold vision, not triangulation, is what will turn out Democratic voters in November.

Patch (Doylestown) — "Bucks Congressional Candidate Hosting Discussion On Affordability Crisis" (April 2026)

Announces a Simonelli-hosted community conversation on the cost-of-living crunch, including healthcare, housing, and utility costs, framed as a contrast event in the closing weeks of the primary.

WHYY — "Pa. election 2026: 1st Congressional District primary voter guide" (April 2026)

Voter guide covering Simonelli alongside Harvie; notes she is the sole Democratic challenger to survive petition filings and is running on Medicare for All, a national public option, and clean-energy policy.

Pennsylvania State Senate

Steve Santarsiero · 10th Senate District · Incumbent

Has represented the 10th District since 2019 and serves as chair of the Bucks County Democrats. Running unopposed in the May primary. Active on minimum wage, PECO rate increases, and other legislative issues.

Central Bucks News / PA House Democrats — "Local lawmakers say they oppose proposed PECO rate hikes" (April 6, 2026)

Santarsiero joins the Bucks County House delegation in formally opposing PECO's proposed gas and electric rate increases, framing the request as unaffordable for working families already squeezed by cost-of-living pressures.

Bucks County Beacon — "Three Bucks County Republican State Lawmakers Voted to Keep Pennsylvania's Minimum Wage at $7.25 Per Hour" (March 2026)

Highlights Santarsiero's continued push to raise the state's minimum wage and contrasts the Bucks Democratic delegation's support with three local Republican lawmakers who voted against the increase.

Eileen Hartnett Albillar · 6th Senate District · Challenger to Sen. Frank Farry

Bucks County Clerk of Courts who launched her bid in January 2026 to unseat Republican Frank Farry. Former Warrington Township supervisor with a background in social work and nonprofit leadership. Endorsed by Senate Democratic Leader Jay Costa, Sen. Santarsiero, Commissioners Harvie and Ellis-Marseglia. Focuses on affordability, SEPTA funding, and working families.

Bucks County Beacon — "Long-Time Bucks County Public Servant Eileen Hartnett Albillar Wants to Help Democrats Flip Pennsylvania's State Senate" (January 2026, still in active rotation)

Launch profile framing Hartnett Albillar's campaign as central to Democrats' hopes of flipping the state Senate; lays out her platform on affordability, transit, and public services.

LevittownNow.com / Yahoo News — "Democratic County Row Officer Launches Bid For State Senate" (January 21, 2026)

Covers her formal entry into the race against Sen. Farry, her slate of opening endorsements, and her emphasis on social-work roots and local-government experience as Warrington supervisor.

16th Senate District — Contested Primary · Mark Pinsley vs. Bradley Merkl-Gump (and Wayne Codner)

Contested primary for one of the most important state Senate flip targets of 2026. Mark Pinsley is the Lehigh County Controller; Bradley Merkl-Gump is a Pennridge School Board member and middle-school history teacher; Wayne Codner is also on the Democratic ballot. The Republican incumbent, Sen. Jarrett Coleman, is unopposed.

Spotlight PA — "Pennsylvania legislative primaries to watch in 2026" (March 2026)

Names SD-16 as one of the pivotal legislative primaries of the year and explains why Democrats see it as their best chance in a generation to chip away at the GOP's narrow state Senate majority.

LehighValleyNews / PoliticsPA — "Pinsley drops out of PA-07 race, shifts focus to state Senate" (February 26, 2026)

Reports Pinsley exiting the crowded PA-07 congressional field to concentrate resources on his third run for SD-16 — a shift that consolidates the primary field but leaves a three-way contest with Merkl-Gump and Codner.

Bucks County Herald — "Contested Democratic primary set in Pennsylvania's 16th Senatorial District" (March 2026)

Breaks down the three-candidate Democratic primary and the strategic stakes for Bucks-adjacent voters, emphasizing the flip-the-Senate math and the contrast between Pinsley's countywide profile and Merkl-Gump's school-board base.

Pennsylvania State House

Bryan Allen · 18th District · Bensalem (challenger to Rep. K.C. Tomlinson)

Former Bensalem Township council member and chief of staff for State Rep. Tina Davis, 47. Introduced the first casino tax rebate for Bensalem residents. Vice chair of Bucks County Water and Sewer Authority Board. Challenging Republican K.C. Tomlinson.

Bucks County Beacon — "Bensalem's Bryan Allen Wants to Help Democrats Expand Their Majority in the Pennsylvania State House" (February 2026)

In-depth profile of Allen's legislative agenda — property tax reform, school funding, and public safety — and how his council experience and tax-rebate track record inform his pitch to swing voters in HD-18.

Bensalem Weekly — "Bensalem 18th District Election 2026: Allen vs. Tomlinson Coverage" (April 2026)

Running coverage of the HD-18 race, recapping Allen's campaign events and Tomlinson's recent defense of her school-funding record as the general election shapes up.

Kristin Egan · 142nd District · Langhorne (challenger to Rep. Joe Hogan)

Neshaminy School Board director and 20-year public school teacher challenging Republican Joe Hogan. First state candidate to publicly oppose $227M PennDOT US-1 reconstruction project. Platform: education funding reform, public safety, cost of living.
