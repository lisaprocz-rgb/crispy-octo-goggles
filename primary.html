Bucks County 2026 Democratic Primary — April 26, 2026

Executive Summary. With just 23 days until polls open on May 19, the marquee Bucks County race remains the PA-01 Democratic primary between Bucks County Commissioner Bob Harvie and Quakertown progressive Lucia Simonelli, who meet Monday night, April 27, at a League of Women Voters forum — the first head-to-head debate of the cycle. New Q1 FEC reports show GOP incumbent Brian Fitzpatrick with roughly $7M more cash on hand than the entire Democratic field combined, sharpening the contrast going into the final stretch. Down ballot, every Bucks County state House Democrat is running unopposed in the primary, while the contested 16th Senate District battle between Lehigh County Controller Mark Pinsley and Pennridge schoolboard member Bradley Merkl-Gump remains the only competitive intra-party fight on the regional ballot.
