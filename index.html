Executive Summary

The May 19 primary is now six days behind us, and the Bucks County ballot for November is set. Democrat Bob Harvie cruised to the PA‑01 congressional nomination with 65% of the vote and will challenge Republican U.S. Rep. Brian Fitzpatrick in what is shaping up to be one of four marquee House races in Pennsylvania. The lone other contested Democratic primary in the region — the 16th state Senate seat — went to Lehigh County Controller Mark Pinsley over Bradley Merkl‑Gump. Every Bucks County Democratic state House and remaining Senate candidate ran unopposed and has advanced directly to the general election.

U.S. Congress — 1st Congressional District

Bob Harvie — Falls Township (PA‑01)

Bucks County Commissioner who announced his congressional bid in April 2025. Lifelong Bucks County resident born in Bristol Borough, graduated magna cum laude from George Washington University, spent 20 years as a teacher and Social Studies Department chair at Bucks County Technical High School. Elected County Commissioner in 2019, named board chair in 2022, re-elected in 2023. Campaign centers on economic fairness and kitchen-table issues.

WHYY News — “Bob Harvie wins Democratic primary for Pa.’s 1st Congressional District” (May 19, 2026)

Harvie won the Democratic nomination with 65% of the vote (45,942) to Lucia Simonelli’s 35% (27,430); the AP called the race less than two hours after polls closed. He told WHYY he is focusing on affordability heading into the November face-off with Fitzpatrick.

The Philadelphia Inquirer — “Bucks County’s Bob Harvie will face Brian Fitzpatrick in the fall as Democrats choose nominees for key Pa. swing seats” (May 19, 2026)

The PA‑01 contest — covering all of Bucks County and a sliver of Montgomery County — is one of four Republican-held seats Pennsylvania Democrats are targeting as they try to retake the U.S. House.

Bucks County Beacon — “DCCC Adds Bob Harvie’s Campaign to Its ‘Red to Blue’ Program” (May 2026)

The Democratic Congressional Campaign Committee added Harvie’s campaign to its “Red to Blue” program, a signal of national party investment in the seat. Gov. Josh Shapiro had endorsed Harvie on May 10.

Lucia Simonelli — Quakertown Borough (PA‑01)

Holds a PhD in mathematics and has served as a scientific advisor in the U.S. Senate. Born in Lansdale, now settled in Quakertown. Directs programs for a nonprofit focused on scaling clean-air technologies. Running as a progressive, zeroing in on universal healthcare and Medicare for All.

Bucks County Herald — “Bob Harvie wins PA‑01 Primary; Lucia Simonelli concedes” (May 19, 2026)

Simonelli, the mathematician and former U.S. Senate climate advisor, conceded on primary night and congratulated Harvie after finishing with 35% of the vote in her progressive, healthcare-focused bid.

16th Senatorial District — Mark Pinsley (Nominee) & Bradley Merkl-Gump

A contested Democratic primary between Mark Pinsley (Lehigh County Controller) and Bradley Merkl-Gump (Pennridge School Board), in a district spanning Upper Bucks and parts of Lehigh County — a key race for flipping the state Senate.

Pinsley defeated Merkl-Gump for the nomination; Merkl-Gump conceded late Tuesday and the AP called the race Wednesday morning. Pinsley will face Republican incumbent Jarrett Coleman in November.
