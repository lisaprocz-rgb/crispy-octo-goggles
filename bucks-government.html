Mail-ballot cure policy

How Bucks notifies voters

Bucks operates a "notice and cure" process — voters with a correctible defect are contacted by postcard, phone, and/or email.

How to cure

Cure in person at the Board of Elections, 55 E. Court Street, Doylestown, OR vote a provisional ballot at the polls.

Standard correctible defects

Missing signature, missing date, naked ballot (no secrecy envelope), or an improperly opened/sealed outer envelope.

Cure deadline + litigation context

The cure deadline is 8 PM on Election Day — Bucks does NOT offer a post-Election-Day cure window. Commissioners Marseglia and Harvie remain associated with the 2024 dispute over counting undated/incorrectly dated ballots, which Republicans continue to cite (see Commissioners section).
