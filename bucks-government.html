Mail-ballot cure policy

Bucks operates a "notice and cure" process. The following is standing reference (no policy change surfaced this week):

How Bucks notifies votersThe county notifies voters of correctible defects by postcard, phone, and email.
How to cureCure in person at the elections office, 55 E. Court St., Doylestown — or vote a provisional ballot at the polls on Election Day.
Standard correctible defectsMissing signature, missing date, naked ballot (no secrecy envelope), improperly sealed/opened envelope.
Cure deadline8:00 PM on Election Day — Bucks runs no post-Election-Day cure window. The undated/misdated-ballot question remains a live litigation theme statewide.
