This Week at a Glance
  • Pollbook pilot OFF: PA Dept. of State postponed its internet-connected e-pollbook pilot — it will not run in the 2026 cycle. Only Philadelphia, Lebanon & Delaware counties had signed up; Bucks was never a participant. (Votebeat/Spotlight PA, May 12–13)
  • DA Khan (June 2): Amended the office's historic housing-justice civil suit against a Dublin Borough landlord, adding four more tenants — including a mother and her 11-year-old son — under the PA Unfair Trade Practices Law.
  • Commissioners (May 21): Unanimously approved a $2,000-per-district "bell-to-bell" incentive encouraging Bucks's 13 school districts + 3 tech schools to ban classroom phone use; Marseglia led the push.
  • Bridge dedication (May 26): Marseglia helped dedicate Bridge #359 in West Rockhill Twp. to Vietnam-era Sgt. John R. Millikan — the 21st county bridge named for a fallen Vietnam soldier since 2022.
  • DA sentencings: A Philadelphia man drew 4½–10 yrs for an Upper Southampton home invasion (June 1, before Judge Bateman); two men were sentenced for statutory sexual assault of a 13-year-old (May 20, Judge Bateman).
  • ICE litigation still live: The ACLU-PA/Community Justice Project appeal of Judge Trauger's ruling on the now-terminated 287(g) agreement continues; Commissioners' May resolution declaring the pact invalid stands.

Mail-ballot CURE policy
How Bucks notifies voters Bucks operates a "notice and cure" process — voters with a correctible defect are notified by postcard, phone, and/or email.
How to cure Cure in person at the Board of Elections, 55 E. Court St., Doylestown — OR vote a provisional ballot at the polls on Election Day.
Standard correctible defects Missing signature, missing date, "naked" ballot (no secrecy envelope), or an improperly opened/sealed outer envelope.
Cure deadline & litigation context Cure deadline is 8 PM on Election Day — Bucks provides no post-Election-Day cure window. The county's posture on undated/misdated ballots remains tied to ongoing statewide litigation; see Standing Watch Items.
