Mail-Ballot Cure Policy

How Bucks notifies: Postcard, phone, and email notification of ballot defects.

How to cure: Cure in person at 55 E. Court St., Doylestown, or vote provisional at the polls on Election Day.

Correctible defects: Missing signature, missing date, naked ballot (no inner envelope), improperly opened envelope.

Cure deadline: 8:00 PM on Election Day. Bucks County does not have a post-Election Day cure window — voters must cure by 8 PM Election Day or their ballot cannot count.
