Mail-ballot cure policy
How Bucks notifies voters Bucks operates a "notice and cure" process: voters with a correctible defect are notified by postcard, phone and/or email so they can fix the ballot.
How to cure Cure in person at the Board of Elections, 55 E. Court Street, Doylestown — OR vote a provisional ballot at your polling place on Election Day.
Standard correctible defects Missing signature, missing date, "naked" ballot (no secrecy/inner envelope), or an improperly opened/sealed outer envelope.
Cure deadline + litigation context Cure deadline is 8 PM on Election Day — Bucks does not offer a post-Election-Day cure window. Undated/misdated mail-ballot rules remain the subject of ongoing statewide litigation in Pennsylvania.
