This Week at a Glance
This week at a glance:
  • ICE-interaction policy now in force. The Commissioners' six-page resolution governing how county employees deal with federal immigration agents took effect May 21 and drew fresh coverage May 28. Passed 2-1 — Marseglia and Harvie for, DiGirolamo against.
  • Sgt. John R. Millikan Bridge dedicated May 26. Bridge #359 became the 21st county-owned bridge named for a Bucks Vietnam War casualty under the Vietnam Veterans Memorial Bridge Program.
  • $6M Delaware Canal repairs celebrated May 26 by county and state officials.

  • Climate-suit appeal heats up. AG Dave Sunday, the U.S. Chamber and a civil-justice coalition filed amicus briefs (early May) backing Judge Corr's dismissal as Bucks's appeal proceeds in Commonwealth Court.

Mail-ballot CURE policy
How Bucks notifies voters Bucks operates a "notice and cure" process: voters with a correctible defect are contacted by postcard, phone, and/or email.
How to cure Cure in person at the Board of Elections, 55 E. Court St., Doylestown — OR vote a provisional ballot at the polls on Election Day.
Standard correctible defects Missing signature, missing date, "naked" ballot (no secrecy envelope), and improperly opened/sealed outer envelope.
Cure deadline & litigation context Cure deadline is 8 PM on Election Day — Bucks has no post-Election-Day cure window. Undated/misdated mail-ballot rules remain the subject of ongoing statewide litigation.
