Mail-ballot cure policy

How Bucks notifies

Bucks operates a "notice and cure" process — voters with a correctible defect are notified by postcard, phone, and email.

How to cure

Cure in person at the Board of Elections, 55 E. Court Street, Doylestown, OR vote a provisional ballot at the polls.

Standard correctible defects

Missing signature, missing date, naked ballot (no secrecy envelope), and an improperly opened/sealed outer envelope.

Cure deadline + context

The cure deadline is 8 PM on Election Day — Bucks does not offer a post-Election-Day cure window. Undated/incorrectly-dated mail ballots remain the subject of ongoing statewide litigation in Pennsylvania.
