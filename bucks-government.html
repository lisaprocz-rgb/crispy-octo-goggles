Ballot Cure Policy — Bucks County "Notice and Cure"

Bucks County operates a "notice and cure" system for mail ballots with defects (missing signatures, undated ballots, etc.). Voters are notified via postcard, phone, and email and can cure in person at the Board of Elections office or vote provisionally at the polls.

Cure deadline: Cure must occur by 8 PM on Election Day. Bucks County does not have a post-Election Day cure window.
Standard correctible defects: Missing signature, missing date, undated ballot, improperly opened envelope, naked ballot.

Climate Lawsuit Appeal — Commonwealth Court
Amicus Briefs Deliver Another Blow to Bucks County's Failing Climate Lawsuit
Energy In Depth | April 2026
Bucks County's climate lawsuit against major oil companies faces renewed legal pressure in Commonwealth Court. In April 2026, PA Attorney General Dave Sunday, the U.S. Chamber of Commerce, and the Pennsylvania Coalition for Civil Justice Reform all filed amicus briefs supporting dismissal of the case. AG Sunday argues that federal law supersedes state and county law on emissions regulation, and that the federal government holds responsibility for addressing global emissions issues.
Read more at Energy In Depth →
Case status: Bucks County filed the lawsuit on March 25, 2024, against major oil companies. Judge Stephen Corr dismissed the case with prejudice on May 2025. Bucks County appealed to Commonwealth Court; that appeal remains pending as of June 2026.
