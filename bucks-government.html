Mail-ballot cure policy

How Bucks notifies voters

Bucks operates a "notice and cure" process — voters with a correctible defect are notified by postcard, phone, and email.

How to cure

Cure in person at the Board of Elections, 55 E. Court St., Doylestown — or vote a provisional ballot at the polls.

Standard correctible defects

Missing signature, missing date, naked ballot (no secrecy envelope), or an improperly opened/sealed outer envelope.

Cure deadline & litigation context

The cure deadline is 8 PM on Election Day — Bucks provides no post-Election-Day cure window. The treatment of undated/misdated ballots remains the subject of ongoing statewide litigation.
