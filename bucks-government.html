6 · Mail-ballot cure policy

How Bucks notifies. Bucks operates a "notice and cure" process: voters whose returned ballots have a correctible defect are notified by postcard, phone, and/or email.

How to cure. Affected voters may cure in person at the Board of Elections, 55 E. Court Street, Doylestown, or vote by provisional ballot at their polling place on Election Day.

Standard correctible defects. Missing signature, missing date, a "naked" ballot (no inner secrecy envelope), or an improperly opened/sealed outer envelope.

Cure deadline. 8:00 PM on Election Day (Nov. 3). Bucks does not offer a post-Election-Day cure window. The treatment of undated/misdated mail ballots remains the subject of ongoing statewide litigation.
