Mail-ballot CURE policy
How Bucks notifies voters "Notice and cure" — voters with a correctible defect are notified by postcard, phone, and/or email.
How to cure Cure in person at 55 E. Court Street, Doylestown, OR vote a provisional ballot at the polls on Election Day.
Standard correctible defects Missing signature, missing date, naked ballot (no secrecy envelope), or an improperly opened/sealed outer envelope.
Cure deadline + litigation context 8 PM on Election Day — Bucks has no post-Election-Day cure window. The undated/misdated-ballot question remains the subject of ongoing statewide litigation.
