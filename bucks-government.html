Mail-ballot "notice and cure" policy
How Bucks notifies voters Bucks operates a notice-and-cure process: voters whose mail ballots have a correctable defect are notified by postcard, phone, and/or email.
How to cure Cure in person at the Board of Elections, 55 E. Court St., Doylestown — or vote by provisional ballot at your polling place on Election Day.
Standard correctable defects Missing signature, missing handwritten date, "naked" ballot (no secrecy envelope), or an improperly sealed/opened outer envelope.
Cure deadline 8:00 p.m. on Election Day. Bucks does not offer a post–Election Day cure window; the undated/misdated-ballot question remains the subject of ongoing statewide litigation.
