Mail-ballot CURE policy
How Bucks notifies voters Bucks operates a "notice and cure" process — voters with a correctible defect are notified by postcard, phone, and/or email.
How to cure Cure in person at the Board of Elections, 55 E. Court St., Doylestown (officials pull the ballot so the voter can sign/date), OR vote a provisional ballot at the polls on Election Day.
Standard correctible defects Missing signature, missing date, naked ballot (no inner secrecy envelope), or an improperly opened/sealed envelope.
Cure deadline 8 PM on Election Day — Bucks has no post-Election-Day cure window. (Undated/misdated-ballot treatment remains the subject of ongoing statewide litigation.)
