6 · Mail-ballot cure policy
How Bucks notifies voters "Notice and cure" — the county notifies affected voters by postcard, phone, and email when a correctible defect is found.
How to cure In person at 55 E. Court St., Doylestown, OR vote a provisional ballot at your polling place on Election Day.
Standard correctible defects Missing signature, missing date, naked ballot (no secrecy envelope), or an improperly opened/sealed envelope.
Cure deadline + litigation context Cure deadline is 8 PM on Election Day — Bucks has no post-Election-Day cure window. Undated/misdated-ballot rules remain the subject of ongoing statewide litigation.
