Mail-ballot cure policy

How Bucks notifies:

"Notice and cure" — voters with a correctible defect are contacted by postcard, phone, and/or email.

How to cure:

In person at 55 E. Court St., Doylestown, or by voting a provisional ballot at the polls.

Standard correctible defects:

Missing signature, missing date, naked ballot (no secrecy envelope), or an improperly opened outer envelope.

Cure deadline:

8:00 PM on Election Day — Bucks has no post-Election-Day cure window. Undated/incorrectly-dated-ballot litigation remains a live statewide issue to watch.
