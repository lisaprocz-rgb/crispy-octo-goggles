Mail-ballot CURE policy
How Bucks notifies voters Bucks operates a "notice and cure" process — voters with a correctible defect are notified by postcard, phone, and/or email.
How to cure Cure in person at the Board of Elections, 55 E. Court St., Doylestown — OR vote a provisional ballot at your polling place on Election Day.
Standard correctible defects Missing signature, missing date, "naked" ballot (no inner secrecy envelope), or an improperly opened/sealed envelope.
Cure deadline + litigation context Cure deadline is 8 PM on Election Day — Bucks has no post-Election-Day cure window. The statewide fight over undated/misdated ballots continues; voting-rights groups argue the dating requirement serves no purpose, while it remains enforced for now.
