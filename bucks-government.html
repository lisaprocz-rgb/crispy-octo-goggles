Mail-ballot cure policy

How Bucks notifies

Bucks operates a "notice and cure" process — voters with a correctible defect are notified by postcard, phone, and email.

How to cure

Cure in person at 55 E. Court Street, Doylestown, or vote a provisional ballot at the polls.

Standard correctible defects

Missing signature, missing date, naked ballot (no secrecy envelope), or an improperly opened/sealed envelope.

Cure deadline

8 PM on Election Day — Bucks does not offer a post-Election-Day cure window.
