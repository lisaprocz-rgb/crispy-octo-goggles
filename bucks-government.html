Mail‑ballot cure policy
How Bucks notifies voters Bucks operates a "notice and cure" process: voters with a correctible defect are notified by postcard, phone and/or email.
How to cure Cure in person at the Board of Elections, 55 E. Court St., Doylestown — or vote a provisional ballot at the polls on Election Day.
Standard correctible defects Missing signature, missing date, naked ballot (no inner secrecy envelope), or an improperly opened / sealed return envelope.
Cure deadline + litigation context Cure deadline is 8 PM on Election Day — Bucks does not offer a post‑Election‑Day cure window. The treatment of undated / misdated mail ballots remains the subject of statewide litigation that has repeatedly reached the courts.
