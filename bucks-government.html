Mail-Ballot Cure Policy

How Bucks notifies voters of defects

Bucks operates a "notice and cure" workflow. When a return envelope is flagged as defective during pre-canvass intake, the Elections Office reaches out by postcard, by phone call, and (when an email address is on file) by email.

How to cure

Two options: (1) come in person to 55 E. Court Street, Doylestown to sign / date / repackage the ballot; or (2) appear at the assigned polling place on Election Day and cast a provisional ballot.

Standard correctible defects

  • Missing voter signature on the outer declaration envelope
  • Missing date or wrongly-dated outer envelope (litigation context: PA Supreme Court ordered counties in Nov 2024 not to count undated/misdated mail ballots; constitutional question on date requirement still unresolved)
  • "Naked" ballot — ballot returned without the secrecy envelope
  • Improperly opened or torn secrecy envelope

Cure deadline + litigation context

Cure deadline in Bucks is 8 PM on Election Day — there is no Bucks-specific post-Election-Day cure window. The undated/misdated-ballot question fueled the November 2024 dispute that drew Marseglia's "court precedent doesn't matter anymore" remark and her subsequent public apology; the broader constitutional challenge to the date requirement is still pending in federal and state courts.
