Mail-ballot cure policy
How Bucks notifies "Notice and cure": voters with a correctible defect are notified by postcard, phone, and/or email.
How to cure Cure in person at 55 E. Court St., Doylestown — OR vote a provisional ballot at your polling place on Election Day.
Standard correctible defects Missing signature, missing date, naked ballot (no secrecy envelope), or an improperly opened/sealed envelope.
Cure deadline + context 8 PM on Election Day — Bucks provides no post-Election-Day cure window. Undated/misdated mail-ballot rules remain the subject of ongoing statewide litigation.
