Mail-ballot cure policy
How Bucks notifies voters Bucks operates a "notice and cure" process — voters with a correctible defect are notified by postcard, phone and/or email.
How to cure Cure in person at 55 E. Court Street, Doylestown, OR vote a provisional ballot at your polling place on Election Day.
Standard correctible defects Missing signature, missing date, "naked" ballot (no inner secrecy envelope), or an improperly opened/sealed outer envelope.
Cure deadline & litigation context Cure deadline is 8 PM on Election Day — Bucks provides no post-Election-Day cure window. Undated/misdated-ballot rules remain the subject of ongoing statewide litigation.
