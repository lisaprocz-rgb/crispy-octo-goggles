Mail-ballot cure policy

How Bucks notifies voters

Bucks operates a "notice and cure" process — voters with a correctible defect are notified by postcard, phone, and/or email.

How to cure

Cure in person at the elections office, 55 E. Court St., Doylestown (also Levittown & Quakertown Government Services Centers), or vote a provisional ballot at your polling place on Election Day.

Standard correctible defects

Missing signature, missing date, naked ballot (no secrecy envelope), or an improperly opened/sealed envelope.

Cure deadline

8:00 PM on Election Day. Bucks has no post-Election-Day cure window. Statewide voter hotline: 833-PA-VOTES.
