Mail-ballot cure policy
How Bucks notifies Bucks operates a "notice and cure" process — voters whose ballots have a correctible defect are notified by postcard, phone and/or email.
How to cure Cure in person at the Board of Elections, 55 E. Court St., Doylestown — or vote a provisional ballot at the polls on Election Day.
Standard correctible defects Missing signature, missing date, "naked" ballot (no secrecy envelope), or an improperly opened/sealed outer envelope.
Cure deadline 8 PM on Election Day (Nov 3) — Bucks offers no post-Election-Day cure window. Undated / misdated ballot handling remains the subject of ongoing statewide litigation.
