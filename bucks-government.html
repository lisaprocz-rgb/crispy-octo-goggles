Mail-ballot cure policy

Bucks operates a "notice and cure" process: voters with a correctible defect are notified by postcard, phone, and email.

  • How to cure: in person at 55 E. Court Street, Doylestown, OR vote provisionally at the polls.
  • Standard correctible defects: missing signature, missing date, naked ballot (no secrecy envelope), improperly opened envelope.
  • Cure deadline: 8 PM on Election Day — Bucks offers no post-Election-Day cure window.
