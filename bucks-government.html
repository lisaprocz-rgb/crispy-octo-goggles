Mail-ballot CURE policy
How Bucks notifies voters "Notice and cure" — the county notifies affected voters by postcard, phone, and email when a returned ballot has a correctible defect.
How to cure In person at 55 E. Court Street, Doylestown, OR by voting a provisional ballot at the polls on Election Day.
Standard correctible defects Missing signature, missing date, naked ballot (no secrecy envelope), or an improperly opened outer envelope.
Cure deadline 8:00 PM on Election Day. Bucks has no post-Election-Day cure window. The scope of undated/misdated-ballot rules remains subject to ongoing PA litigation.
