6 · Mail-ballot cure policy

How Bucks notifies: Bucks operates a "notice and cure" process — voters whose mail ballots have a correctible defect are notified by postcard, phone, and/or email.

How to cure: In person at the election office, 55 E. Court St., Doylestown — or vote a provisional ballot at the polls on Election Day.

Standard correctible defects: missing signature, missing date on the outer envelope, "naked" ballot (no secrecy envelope), or an improperly opened/sealed envelope.

Cure deadline: 8 p.m. on Election Day (Nov. 3). Bucks does not offer a post–Election Day cure window.
