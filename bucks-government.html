Mail-ballot cure policy

How Bucks notifies: "Notice and cure" — voters with a correctible defect are contacted by postcard, phone, and/or email.

How to cure: In person at 55 E. Court St., Doylestown, OR by voting a provisional ballot at the polls.

Standard correctible defects: missing signature, missing date, naked ballot (no secrecy envelope), or improperly opened envelope.

Cure deadline: 8 PM on Election Day — Bucks operates no post-Election-Day cure window. The undated/incorrectly-dated-ballot question remains the subject of ongoing statewide litigation.
