Mail-ballot cure policy
How Bucks notifies voters

Bucks operates a "notice and cure" process — voters with a correctible defect are notified by postcard, phone and/or email.

How to cure

Cure in person at 55 E. Court St., Doylestown, OR vote by provisional ballot at your polling place.

Standard correctible defects

Missing signature, missing date, naked ballot (missing secrecy envelope), or improperly opened return envelope.

Cure deadline + context

Cure deadline is 8 PM on Election Day. Bucks has no post-Election-Day cure window. The Marseglia/Harvie posture on undated-ballot counting remains a recurring flashpoint (see Standing Watch Items).

PA internet-connected pollbook pilot — watch
Pilot postponed — Bucks not a participant

The PA Department of State postponed its internet-connected e-pollbook pilot; it did not run in the May primary. Only Philadelphia, Lebanon and Delaware counties had signed up; Bucks was never confirmed as a participant. The department cited unresolved technical questions and low county interest, delaying the test until at least next year.
