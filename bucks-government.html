Mail-ballot cure policy
How Bucks notifies voters Bucks operates a "notice and cure" process — voters with a correctible defect are notified by postcard, phone and/or email.
How to cure Cure in person at the Board of Elections, 55 E. Court St., Doylestown, or vote a provisional ballot at the polls on Election Day.
Standard correctible defects Missing signature, missing date, a "naked" ballot (no secrecy envelope), or an improperly sealed/opened outer envelope.
Cure deadline & litigation context Cure deadline is 8 p.m. on Election Day — Bucks offers no post-Election-Day cure window. The treatment of missing/undated signatures remains the subject of ongoing statewide litigation.
