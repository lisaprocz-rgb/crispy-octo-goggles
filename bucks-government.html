📋 This Week at a Glance
This week at a glance:
  • Today is Pennsylvania Primary Election Day — Bucks polls open 7 AM to 8 PM. Vice-Chair Bob Harvie (D) is on the ballot in the Democratic primary for U.S. House PA-1 against Lucia Simonelli; Gov. Shapiro has endorsed Harvie and prediction markets give him a ~90% probability.

  • Acting Treasurer Mark Moffa remains unconfirmed by the PA Senate; Acting Register of Wills Douglas Wayne is still First Deputy filling Linda Bobrin's seat after she took the bench Jan. 6.

🩹 Mail-Ballot Cure Policy
How Bucks notifies voters Bucks operates a "notice and cure" model. The Board of Elections proactively notifies voters of correctible defects by postcard, phone, and email — coordinated with party-led tracker programs.
How to cure Two paths: (1) come in person to 55 E. Court Street, Doylestown and fix the defective ballot at the BOE; or (2) report to your polling place and vote a provisional ballot, which is then adjudicated.
Standard correctible defects Missing voter signature on declaration · missing date on declaration · "naked" ballot (no secrecy envelope) · improperly opened or broken-seal return envelope.
Cure deadline & litigation context Cure must be completed by 8 PM Election Day (today). Bucks does not have a post-Election Day cure window. The undated-ballot litigation pending in PA appellate courts continues to be the relevant macro backdrop.
