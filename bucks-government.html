Mail-ballot cure policy

How Bucks notifies voters

Bucks operates a "notice and cure" process — voters whose mail ballots have a correctable defect are notified by postcard, phone, and email.

How to cure

Cure in person at the Board of Elections, 55 E. Court St., Doylestown, or vote by provisional ballot at the polls on Election Day.

Standard correctable defects

Missing signature, missing date, naked ballot (no secrecy envelope), or an improperly opened/sealed outer envelope.

Cure deadline & litigation context

The cure deadline is 8 PM on Election Day — Bucks has no post-Election-Day cure window. Undated and misdated mail ballots remain a recurring point of GOP litigation against the Board of Elections; a federal appeals court has previously held that misdated ballots remain valid. Federal appeals court says misdated ballots still valid (via Yahoo/AP — background).
