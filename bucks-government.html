6 · Mail-ballot cure policy
How Bucks notifies voters Bucks operates a "notice and cure" process — voters with a correctible defect are notified by postcard, phone, and/or email.
How to cure Cure in person at 55 E. Court St., Doylestown — OR vote a provisional ballot at your polling place on Election Day.
Standard correctible defects Missing signature · missing date · naked ballot (no secrecy envelope) · improperly opened/sealed outer envelope.
Cure deadline & litigation context Cure deadline is 8 PM on Election Day — Bucks has no post-Election-Day cure window. The undated/misdated-ballot question remains a recurring statewide litigation flashpoint.
