Mail-ballot cure policy

How Bucks notifies: Bucks operates a "notice and cure" process — voters with a correctible defect are notified by postcard, phone, and/or email.

How to cure: In person at the Board of Elections, 55 E. Court St., Doylestown — or vote a provisional ballot at the polls on Election Day.

Standard correctible defects: missing signature, missing date, naked ballot (no secrecy envelope), improperly opened/sealed envelope.

Cure deadline: 8 PM on Election Day (Nov 3). Bucks has no post-Election-Day cure window.
