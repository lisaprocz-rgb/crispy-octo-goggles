Mail-ballot cure policy
How Bucks notifies voters Bucks operates "notice and cure" — voters with a correctible defect are notified by postcard, phone, and/or email.
How to cure Cure in person at 55 E. Court St., Doylestown — OR vote a provisional ballot at the polls.
Standard correctible defects Missing signature, missing date, naked ballot (no secrecy envelope), or an improperly opened/sealed outer envelope.
Cure deadline 8 PM on Election Day. Bucks does NOT offer a post-Election-Day cure window. Provisional voting at the polls remains the fallback for an unfixed defect.
