Mail-ballot cure policy
How Bucks notifiesBucks operates "notice and cure": voters whose ballots have correctible defects are contacted by postcard, phone, and email.
How to cureCure in person at the BOE, 55 E. Court St., Doylestown — or vote by provisional ballot at your polling place on Election Day.
Standard correctible defectsMissing signature · missing date · "naked ballot" (no secrecy envelope) · improperly opened envelope.
Cure deadline + litigation context8 PM on Election Day — Bucks has no post-Election Day cure window. Statewide litigation over undated/misdated ballots continues to shape what counts; watch the commissioners' posture (Standing Watch Item #6).
