6 · Mail-ballot CURE policy

How Bucks notifies: Bucks operates a "notice and cure" process — voters whose returned ballots have a correctible defect are notified by postcard, phone, and/or email.

How to cure: Correct the issue in person at 55 E. Court St., Doylestown, or vote by provisional ballot at your polling place on Election Day.

Standard correctible defects: missing signature, missing/incorrect date, "naked" ballot (no secrecy envelope), or an improperly sealed/opened outer envelope.

Cure deadline: 8 PM on Election Day (Nov. 3) — Bucks has no post-Election-Day cure window. The scope of required notice/cure remains the subject of ongoing statewide litigation.
