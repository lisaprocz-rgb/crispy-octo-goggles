📌 This week at a glance:
  • Primary Day was Tuesday, May 19. Vice-Chair Commissioner Bob Harvie (D) won the PA-01 Democratic congressional primary 65%–35% over Lucia Simonelli and advances to face Republican Rep. Brian Fitzpatrick in November.

  • Clerk of Courts Eileen Hartnett Albillar (D) ran unopposed in the SD-6 Democratic primary and advances to challenge GOP incumbent Sen. Frank Farry in the fall.
  • In neighboring SD-16 (which crosses into Bucks), Lehigh County Controller Mark Pinsley won the Democratic primary over Bradley Merkl-Gump.

Mail-ballot CURE policy
How Bucks notifies voters Bucks operates a "notice and cure" process: voters with a correctible defect are notified by postcard, phone and/or email.
How to cure Cure in person at the Board of Elections, 55 E. Court St., Doylestown — OR vote a provisional ballot at your polling place on Election Day.
Standard correctible defects Missing signature, missing date, "naked" ballot (no secrecy envelope), and improperly opened/sealed return envelope.
Cure deadline 8 p.m. on Election Day. Bucks does not offer a post-Election-Day cure window. The undated/incorrectly-dated-ballot question remains the subject of ongoing statewide litigation.
