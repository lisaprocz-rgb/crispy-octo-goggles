Mail-ballot cure policy
How Bucks notifies voters Bucks operates a "notice and cure" program — voters with a correctible defect are notified by postcard, phone, and email.
How to cure Cure in person at 55 E. Court St., Doylestown — OR vote a provisional ballot at the polls on Election Day.
Standard correctible defects Missing signature, missing date, "naked" ballot (no inner secrecy envelope), or an improperly opened/sealed outer envelope.
Cure deadline 8 PM on Election Day — Bucks does not offer a post-Election-Day cure window. Undated/misdated-ballot treatment remains contested in ongoing PA litigation.

Pilot postponed — Bucks not participating The PA Department of State announced (Feb 2026) a pilot to connect e-pollbooks to the internet for the May 19 primary. In mid-May 2026 the department postponed the pilot until at least 2027, citing unresolved technical questions and low county interest — only Philadelphia, Lebanon and Delaware counties had signed up. Bucks was never among them. Watch item resolved for this cycle.
