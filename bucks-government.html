Mail-ballot cure policy

How Bucks notifies voters. Bucks operates a "notice and cure" process: voters whose returned mail ballots have a correctible defect are contacted by postcard, phone, and/or email.

How to cure. A voter can cure in person at the Board of Elections, 55 E. Court St., Doylestown, or vote a provisional ballot at their polling place on Election Day.

Standard correctible defects. Missing signature, missing date, "naked" ballot (no secrecy envelope), or an improperly opened/sealed outer envelope.

Cure deadline. 8 PM on Election Day — Bucks does not operate a post-Election-Day cure window. The undated/incorrectly dated mail-ballot question remains the subject of ongoing statewide litigation.
