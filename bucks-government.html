Mail-ballot CURE policy
How Bucks notifies voters Bucks operates a "notice and cure" process — voters with correctible defects are contacted by postcard, phone, and/or email.
How to cure Cure in person at 55 E. Court Street, Doylestown, OR vote by provisional ballot at the polls on Election Day.
Standard correctible defects Missing signature, missing date, naked ballot (missing secrecy envelope), or an improperly opened outer envelope.
Cure deadline & litigation context Cure deadline is 8:00 PM on Election Day — Bucks has no post-Election-Day cure window. The county's posture on undated/mis-dated ballots remains a live statewide legal issue.
