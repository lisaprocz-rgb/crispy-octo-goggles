Mail-ballot cure policy
How Bucks notifies "Notice and cure" — voters with a correctible defect are contacted by postcard, phone, and/or email.
How to cure Cure in person at 55 E. Court St., Doylestown, OR vote a provisional ballot at your polling place on Election Day.
Standard correctible defects Missing signature, missing date, naked ballot (no secrecy envelope), or an improperly opened/sealed envelope.
Cure deadline & litigation context Deadline is 8 PM on Election Day — Bucks operates no post-Election-Day cure window. Undated/misdated mail-ballot disputes remain active statewide in Pennsylvania courts.
