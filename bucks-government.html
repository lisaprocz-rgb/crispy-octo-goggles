Mail-ballot cure policy
How Bucks notifies voters Bucks operates a “notice and cure” process: voters whose mail ballots have correctable defects are notified by postcard, phone and/or email.
How to cure Cure in person at the Board of Elections, 55 E. Court Street, Doylestown — or vote a provisional ballot at the polls on Election Day.
Standard correctable defects Missing signature, missing date, “naked” ballot (no secrecy envelope), or an improperly opened/sealed outer envelope.
Cure deadline & litigation context The cure deadline is 8 PM on Election Day — Bucks provides no post-Election-Day cure window. Undated/misdated mail-ballot rules remain the subject of ongoing statewide litigation.
