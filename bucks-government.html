6 · Mail-ballot cure policy
How Bucks notifies Bucks operates a "notice and cure" process: voters with a correctible defect are notified by postcard, phone, and/or email.
How to cure Cure in person at the elections office, 55 E. Court St., Doylestown, OR vote a provisional ballot at the polls on Election Day.
Standard correctible defects Missing signature, missing date, naked ballot (no secrecy envelope), or an improperly opened/sealed outer envelope.
Cure deadline & litigation context Cure deadline is 8 p.m. on Election Day — Bucks has no post-Election-Day cure window. The treatment of undated/misdated mail ballots remains the subject of ongoing statewide litigation, and figured in the Oct 2026 petition against Commissioner Marseglia.
