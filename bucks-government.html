Mail-ballot cure policy
How Bucks notifies voters Bucks operates a "notice and cure" process: voters with a deficient ballot are notified by postcard, phone, and/or email.
How to cure Cure in person at the Board of Elections, 55 E. Court St., Doylestown — or vote a provisional ballot at your polling place on Election Day.
Standard correctible defects Missing signature, missing date, a "naked" ballot (no secrecy envelope), or an improperly opened/sealed envelope.
Cure deadline & litigation context The cure deadline is 8 p.m. on Election Day (Nov. 3) — Bucks has no post-Election-Day cure window. The handling of undated/misdated mail ballots remains the subject of ongoing statewide litigation.
