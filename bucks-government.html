Mail-ballot cure policy
How Bucks notifies voters of a defective ballot The BOE operates a "notice and cure" practice — voters are contacted by postcard, phone, and email when the office detects a correctible defect.
How to cure a defective ballot In person at 55 E. Court Street, Doylestown or by appearing at the polling place and casting a provisional ballot.
Standard correctible defects Missing signature on the declaration envelope · missing date on the declaration · "naked" ballot (no secrecy envelope) · improperly opened/resealed envelope.
Cure deadline + litigation context 8 PM on Election Day — Bucks does not operate a post-Election-Day cure window. Marseglia and Harvie continue to defend the practice publicly; standing watch item remains open.
