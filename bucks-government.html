This Week at a Glance
This week at a glance:
  • Vice-Chair Bob Harvie (D) won the U.S. House PA-1 seat, ousting five-term Rep. Brian Fitzpatrick (R) 52.7%–47.3% — AP called it early Wed., Nov. 4. Fitzpatrick had carried the district by 13 points in 2024.
  • Harvie's win sets up a pending vacancy on the three-member Board of Commissioners and in his role as Board of Elections chair once he resigns to be sworn into Congress in early January 2027. No resignation date or replacement has been announced as of this run.
  • A judge rejected the Fitzpatrick campaign's pre-election petition to remove Commissioner Diane Ellis-Marseglia (D) from the Board of Elections over her public support for Harvie; the petition was struck down Monday, Nov. 3 — just before polls opened.
  • Bucks drew among the highest early/mail turnout in Pennsylvania heading into the Nov. 3 midterm, per county and statewide ballot trackers.
  • Treasurer Mark Moffa (D) remains acting pending state Senate confirmation; Register of Wills is still being run by acting First Deputy Douglas Wayne (D) after Linda Bobrin's move to the bench — no update this week on either.
  • All nine row offices and the three-seat commission remain in Democratic hands; the Court of Common Pleas retains its Democratic majority after January's historic sweep.

Mail-ballot cure policy
How Bucks notifies The county operates "notice and cure": voters with a correctible defect are notified by postcard, phone, and email.
How to cure Cure in person at the Election Bureau, 55 E. Court St., Doylestown, or vote a provisional ballot at the polls on Election Day.
Standard correctible defects Missing signature, missing date, naked ballot (no secrecy envelope), or an improperly opened/sealed outer envelope.
Cure deadline & litigation context Cure deadline is 8 p.m. on Election Day — Bucks offers no post-Election-Day cure window. The county's undated/dated-envelope and early-voting-access practices have repeatedly drawn litigation in recent cycles; no new cure-policy ruling surfaced this week.
