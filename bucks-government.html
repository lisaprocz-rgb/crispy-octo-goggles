Mail-ballot cure policy
How Bucks notifies voters Bucks operates a "notice and cure" process — voters with a correctible defect are contacted by postcard, phone, and/or email.
How to cure In person at 55 E. Court St., Doylestown, OR by voting a provisional ballot at the polls on Election Day.
Standard correctible defects Missing signature, missing date, naked ballot (no secrecy envelope), or an improperly opened/sealed outer envelope.
Cure deadline & litigation context Cure deadline is 8 PM on Election Day — Bucks provides no post-Election-Day cure window. Undated/mis-dated ballot rules remain the subject of ongoing statewide litigation.
