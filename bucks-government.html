This week at a glance:
  • Post-primary: The May 19 primary is decided — Commissioner Bob Harvie (D) won the PA-1 Democratic congressional primary with ~65% over Lucia Simonelli (~35%) and will face GOP Rep. Brian Fitzpatrick in November.
  • Mail ballots: Bucks reported an unofficial 51,452 absentee/mail-in ballots received by the 8 PM Election Day deadline. Canvass/certification work continues.
  • May 26: Chair Diane Ellis-Marseglia spoke at the dedication of Bucks County Bridge #359 honoring U.S. Army Sgt. John R. Millikan (West Rockhill Twp).
  • School cellphones: Commissioners unanimously approved a $2,000 incentive for any Bucks school district that adopts a "bell-to-bell" phone ban.
  • Redistricting: Commissioners approved a resolution opposing any split of Bucks County across two congressional districts.
  • E-pollbook pilot — POSTPONED: PA Dept. of State delayed its internet-connected pollbook pilot until at least 2027. Bucks had not signed up; only Philadelphia, Lebanon and Delaware counties had.
  • Senior Games: The 43rd Annual Bucks County Senior Games run June 1–6, with a kickoff May 27.

Mail-ballot cure policy

How Bucks notifies

Bucks operates "notice and cure" — voters with a correctible defect are notified by postcard, phone, and/or email.

How to cure

Cure in person at 55 E. Court St., Doylestown, OR vote a provisional ballot at the polls.

Standard correctible defects

Missing signature, missing date, naked ballot (no secrecy envelope), or improperly opened/sealed envelope.

Cure deadline

8 PM on Election Day — Bucks does not offer a post-Election-Day cure window.
