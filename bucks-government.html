Mail-ballot cure policy

How Bucks notifies. Bucks operates a "notice and cure" process — voters with a correctible defect are contacted by postcard, phone, and/or email.

How to cure. In person at 55 E. Court St., Doylestown, or by voting a provisional ballot at the polls on Election Day.

Standard correctible defects. Missing signature, missing date, naked ballot (no secrecy envelope), or an improperly opened/sealed envelope.

Cure deadline. 8 PM on Election Day — Bucks has no post-Election-Day cure window. (The undated/misdated-ballot question remains the subject of ongoing statewide litigation.)
