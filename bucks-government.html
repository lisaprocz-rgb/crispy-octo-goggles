🟢 This week at a glance
  • Bob Harvie (D), the sitting Bucks County Commissioner and Vice Chair, won election to the U.S. House in PA-1, defeating five-term Republican Brian Fitzpatrick 52.7%–47.3% (margin ~5.4 pts). The AP called it at 12:37 a.m. on Nov 4. Harvie still holds his commissioner seat and the Board of Elections chairmanship until he is sworn into Congress in January — setting up a looming commissioner/BOE-chair vacancy.
  • Clerk of Courts Eileen Hartnett Albillar (D) won the PA Senate 6th District, unseating Republican incumbent Frank Farry — meaning a second row-office vacancy (Clerk of Courts) is on the horizon when she takes her Senate seat in January.
  • A Bucks County judge rejected Brian Fitzpatrick's pre-election bid to remove Commissioner Diane Ellis-Marseglia (D) from the Board of Elections; the ruling came down the Monday before Election Day, leaving the Democratic-majority board intact for the Nov 3 count.
  • Gov. Josh Shapiro (D) won reelection, and Democrats flipped all four Pennsylvania swing U.S. House seats (Harvie's PA-1 among them) plus took control in Harrisburg — a "blue trifecta."
  • Locally, Democrats flipped State House seats HD-18 (Bryan Allen over KC Tomlinson), HD-142 (Kristin Egan over Joe Hogan) and HD-143 (Tim Hayes), moving Bucks' House delegation to 8–2 Democratic.
  • The county's Nov 3 general-election canvass and certification is underway; statutory deadlines for computing and certifying returns run through late November. Commissioners' next regular meeting is Wed, Nov 18.
  • The county's climate-deception lawsuit against big oil (dismissed by Judge Stephen Corr) remains on appeal; the county has vowed to pursue the "flawed" ruling in Commonwealth Court. No new ruling surfaced this week.

Mail-ballot cure policy

How Bucks notifies: "notice and cure" — voters with correctible defects are notified by postcard, phone and/or email.

How to cure: in person at 55 E. Court Street, Doylestown, or by voting a provisional ballot at the polls.

Standard correctible defects: missing signature, missing date, naked ballot (no secrecy envelope), improperly opened/sealed envelope.

Cure deadline: 8 PM on Election Day — Bucks offers no post-Election-Day cure window.
