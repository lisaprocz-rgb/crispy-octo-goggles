This Week at a Glance
  • DA Khan wins first-in-PA civil injunction against a smoke shop. On Sept. 2, DA Joe Khan obtained an emergency court order halting tobacco and kratom sales to minors at Zaza Vape & Cigar in Buckingham — believed to be the first time a PA DA has used a civil consumer-protection suit to stop illegal smoke-shop sales. A judge extended the injunction days later.
  • Khan + Chester County DA press the state on kratom. On Sept. 1 (the first day of school), Khan and DA Christopher de Barrena-Sarobe urged PA Health Secretary Dr. Debra Bogen to use emergency powers to schedule 7-OH kratom derivatives as controlled substances.
  • Board of Elections expands to 7 on-demand offices. At its Sept. 9 meeting, the BOE approved four temporary satellite offices (opening early October) atop the three permanent sites — seven locations where voters can apply for and receive a mail ballot in one visit ahead of the Nov. 3 general.
  • Neighborhood First cuts ribbon on new Bristol Twp. space. On Sept. 3, Chair Diane Ellis-Marseglia helped open a 2,300-sq-ft modular addition for the county-backed at-risk-youth program.
  • Entries open for the county's 50th Poet Laureate ($500 honorarium + commissioners' plaque); submissions due Sept. 25. The county is also hosting Medicare Open Enrollment library sessions Sept. 16, 24 & 28.
  • Vice Chair Bob Harvie (D) remains a sitting commissioner while challenging Rep. Brian Fitzpatrick (R) in PA-1, having won the May Democratic primary over Lucia Simonelli.
  • ICE watch: the county's Feb. 18 resolution opposing warehouse detention still stands; the Bensalem and Middletown warehouse owners are not moving forward with conversions. PA's internet-connected pollbook pilot is postponed to at least 2027 (Bucks never signed up).

Mail-ballot cure policy
How Bucks notifies voters Bucks operates a "notice and cure" system: voters with a correctible defect are notified by postcard, phone, and/or email.
How to cure Cure in person at 55 E. Court St., Doylestown, OR vote a provisional ballot at your polling place on Election Day.
Standard correctible defects Missing signature, missing date, naked ballot (no inner secrecy envelope), or an improperly opened/sealed envelope.
Cure deadline + litigation context Cure deadline is 8 PM on Election Day — Bucks provides no post-Election-Day cure window. The treatment of undated/misdated ballots remains subject to ongoing statewide litigation.
