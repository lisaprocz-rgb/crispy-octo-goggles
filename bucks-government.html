Mail-in ballot CURE policy
How Bucks notifies voters Notice-and-cure: postcard, phone call, and email when a ballot is flagged for a correctible defect.
How to cure In person at 55 E. Court Street, Doylestown — call 215-348-6154 in advance so staff can have your ballot ready. Or vote provisional at your polling place.
Standard correctible defects Missing signature on the declaration · missing date · "naked" ballot (no secrecy envelope) · improperly opened return envelope.
Cure deadline + litigation context Cure must be completed by 8 PM Election Day. No post-Election Day cure window in Bucks. Per the Nov 2024 PA Supreme Court ruling, undated/wrongly dated mail ballots will NOT be counted; commissioners and the BOE have stated they will follow that ruling.
