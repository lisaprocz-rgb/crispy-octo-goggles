This Week at a Glance
This week at a glance:
  • Commissioner Bob Harvie (D) is now a U.S. Representative-elect. He defeated five-term Rep. Brian Fitzpatrick (R) in PA-1 on Nov. 3; the AP called it Nov. 4. He keeps his Bucks commissioner seat and Board of Elections chairmanship until he is sworn into Congress in January 2027.

  • A judge rejected Fitzpatrick's bid to remove Commissioner Diane Ellis-Marseglia (D) from the Board of Elections the day before the election (Nov. 2). Out-of-county Judge Jeffrey Sommer found no statutory basis to disqualify her.
  • Two county row officers are now headed elsewhere in January: Harvie to Congress and Albillar to the state Senate — both seats will need successors named.
  • Standing items held steady: Sheriff Ceisler's county has stayed out of the 287(g) ICE program; the climate appeal is still pending at Commonwealth Court; the state's internet-pollbook pilot remains postponed.

Mail-ballot cure policy
How Bucks notifies voters "Notice and cure": the county notifies voters of a correctible defect by postcard, phone, and/or email.
How to cure Cure in person at the Board of Elections, 55 E. Court St., Doylestown — or vote a provisional ballot at the polls.
Standard correctible defects Missing signature, missing date, naked ballot (no secrecy envelope), or an improperly opened/sealed envelope.
Cure deadline & litigation context Cure deadline is 8 PM on Election Day — Bucks has no post-Election-Day cure window. The handling of undated/misdated ballots remains a recurring flashpoint: in the Nov. 2, 2026 hearing, Fitzpatrick's team cited Commissioner Ellis-Marseglia's 2024 remark about counting undated ballots as alleged evidence of bias; the judge declined to remove her.
