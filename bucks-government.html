This Week at a Glance
Where things stand this week:
  • Vice-Chair Bob Harvie (D) is Washington-bound. He defeated U.S. Rep. Brian Fitzpatrick (R) in PA-01 on Nov. 3, flipping a seat the GOP held for over a decade. The new Congress is sworn in Jan. 3, 2027, which will open a Bucks County commissioner vacancy.
  • The commissioner seat will be filled by the Court of Common Pleas, not a special election — a Democrat (matching Harvie's registration) for the balance of the term that ends January 2028. As of early November the court had not finalized the application process or a vote date, and the Democratic Party had not named a replacement ("not yet," per Chair Steve Santarsiero).
  • Fitzpatrick's bid to remove Chair Diane Ellis-Marseglia (D) from the Board of Elections was denied. Chester County Senior Judge Jeffrey Sommer rejected the emergency petition on/around Nov. 2, leaving the Democratic majority on the election board intact.
  • Clerk of Courts Eileen Hartnett Albillar (D) narrowly unseated Sen. Frank Farry (R) in the 6th state Senate District. Democrats captured the state Senate majority and a Harrisburg "trifecta," foreshadowing a second Bucks row-office vacancy once she is seated.
  • Row offices stay solidly Democratic. DA Joe Khan, Sheriff Danny Ceisler and the full 2026 class continue in office; Treasurer (Mark Moffa) and Register of Wills (Douglas Wayne) remain in acting status.
  • No new rulings of note from the Court of Common Pleas this week; President Judge Raymond McHugh is central to the pending commissioner appointment.

Mail-ballot cure policy
How Bucks handles correctable ballot defects How voters are notified: Bucks runs a "notice and cure" program — voters with a flagged ballot are contacted by postcard, phone, and/or email.

How to cure: in person at the Board of Elections, 55 E. Court St., Doylestown, OR by voting a provisional ballot at the polls.

Standard correctable defects: missing signature, missing date, naked ballot (no secrecy envelope), or an improperly sealed/opened outer envelope.

Cure deadline: 8:00 PM on Election Day. Bucks does not offer a post-Election-Day cure window. This posture tracks ongoing statewide litigation over undated/misdated mail ballots.
