Mail-ballot CURE policy

How Bucks notifies: Bucks operates a "notice and cure" system — voters with a defective ballot are contacted by postcard, phone, and/or email.

How to cure: In person at the Board of Elections, 55 E. Court St., Doylestown, OR by voting a provisional ballot at the polls on Election Day.

Standard correctible defects: missing signature, missing date, naked ballot (no secrecy envelope), and improperly opened/sealed outer envelope.

Cure deadline: 8:00 PM on Election Day — Bucks has no post-Election-Day cure window. Undated/incorrectly-dated ballot litigation continues statewide.
