This Week at a Glance
This week at a glance:
  • DA Joe Khan expanded the county's federal Big Tech lawsuit on June 8, adding Roblox, Discord, Meta and X Corp. — making Bucks the first DA's office in the U.S. to sue X.
  • Alec Magill, 32, of Upper Southampton was sentenced to the maximum county term June 5 in a Roblox child-exploitation case before President Judge Raymond F. McHugh.
  • Judge Stephen A. Corr sentenced two Philadelphia men (Sevean Brown, Hassan Nelson) to 4–8 years each June 8 for a Northampton Township "honeytrap" home invasion.
  • Bucks County Detectives warned residents June 11 of a Medicare phone scam spoofing the county's own number.
  • Board of Elections: the May 19 primary is certified; a record 51,452 mail/absentee ballots were received by the 8 PM deadline.
  • Pollbook watch: PA's internet-connected e-pollbook pilot was postponed (May) — Bucks never enrolled; only Philadelphia, Lebanon and Delaware had signed up.
  • Climate appeal: the county's appeal remains pending in Commonwealth Court, with AG Dave Sunday and business groups filing amicus briefs backing dismissal.

Mail-ballot cure policy
How Bucks notifies voters "Notice and cure" — voters with a correctible defect are contacted by postcard, phone, and/or email.
How to cure Cure in person at 55 E. Court Street, Doylestown, OR vote a provisional ballot at the polls on Election Day.
Standard correctible defects Missing signature, missing date, naked ballot (no secrecy envelope), or an improperly opened/sealed outer envelope.
Cure deadline + litigation context Deadline is 8 PM on Election Day — Bucks has no post-Election-Day cure window. Undated/misdated-ballot rules remain contested statewide in ongoing PA litigation.
