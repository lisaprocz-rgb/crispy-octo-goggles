Mail-ballot cure policy
How Bucks notifies "Notice and cure": voters with a correctible defect are notified by postcard, phone, and/or email.
How to cure In person at 55 E. Court Street, Doylestown — or vote a provisional ballot at your polling place on Election Day.
Standard correctible defects Missing signature, missing date, naked ballot (no inner secrecy envelope), improperly opened/sealed envelope.
Cure deadline 8 PM on Election Day. Bucks has no post-Election-Day cure window. Undated/mis-dated ballot treatment remains subject to ongoing statewide litigation.
