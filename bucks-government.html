Mail-ballot cure policy
How Bucks notifies voters Bucks operates a "notice and cure" process: voters with a correctible defect are notified by postcard, phone, and/or email.
How to cure Cure in person at 55 E. Court St., Doylestown, OR vote a provisional ballot at the polls.
Standard correctible defects Missing signature, missing date, naked ballot (no secrecy envelope), or an improperly opened/sealed outer envelope.
Cure deadline & litigation context Cure deadline is 8 PM on Election Day — Bucks has no post-Election-Day cure window. The county's undated/mis-dated-ballot posture has been the subject of repeated litigation, most recently the Fitzpatrick election-board suit (see Standing Watch).
