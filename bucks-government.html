This Week at a Glance
Top developments — where things stand:

  • Democrats flipped the Pennsylvania Senate majority and won control of the U.S. House on Nov. 3; Bucks sat at the center of the statewide "blue wave."
  • 2027 county budget season is underway. Last year's 2026 budget carried an ~8% property-tax increase adopted Dec. 17, 2025; preliminary 2027 figures have not been published yet this run.
  • Next Commissioners' meeting: Wednesday, Dec. 3, 55 E. Court St., Doylestown (livestreamed via CivicClerk). No Board of Elections meeting is currently posted.

Mail-ballot cure policy

How Bucks notifies: the county operates "notice and cure" — voters with a correctable defect are notified by postcard, phone, and email.

How to cure: in person at 55 E. Court St., Doylestown, or by voting a provisional ballot at the polls.

Standard correctable defects: missing signature, missing date, naked ballot (no inner secrecy envelope), or an improperly opened envelope.

Cure deadline: 8 PM on Election Day — Bucks has no post-Election-Day cure window. The statewide fight over undated and misdated ballots continues in the courts.
