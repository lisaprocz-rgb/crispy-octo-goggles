Mail-ballot cure policy
How Bucks notifies voters Bucks operates a "notice and cure" process — voters with a correctible defect are notified by postcard, phone, and/or email. How to cure Cure in person at 55 E. Court St., Doylestown, OR cast a provisional ballot at the polls on Election Day. Standard correctible defects Missing signature · missing date · "naked" ballot (no secrecy envelope) · improperly opened/sealed outer envelope. Cure deadline + context Cure deadline is 8 PM on Election Day — Bucks does not offer a post-Election-Day cure window. Undated/misdated-ballot treatment remains the subject of ongoing statewide litigation.
