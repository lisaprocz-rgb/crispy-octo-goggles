Mail-ballot CURE policy
How Bucks notifies voters Bucks operates a "notice and cure" process — voters with a correctible defect are notified by postcard, phone, and/or email.
How to cure Cure in person at 55 E. Court St., Doylestown, OR vote a provisional ballot at your polling place on Election Day.
Standard correctible defects Missing signature · missing date · "naked" ballot (no secrecy envelope) · improperly opened/sealed envelope.
Cure deadline + context Cure deadline is 8 PM on Election Day — Bucks provides no post-Election-Day cure window. Undated/misdated mail-ballot rules remain the subject of ongoing statewide litigation.
