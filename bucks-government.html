Mail-ballot cure policy

How Bucks notifies voters: Bucks operates a "notice and cure" process — voters whose ballots have correctable defects are contacted by postcard, phone and/or email.

How to cure: In person at the Board of Elections, 55 E. Court St., Doylestown — or vote a provisional ballot at your polling place on Election Day.

Standard correctable defects: missing signature, missing handwritten date, "naked" ballot (no secrecy envelope), improperly sealed/opened outer envelope.

Cure deadline: 8 p.m. on Election Day (Nov. 3). Bucks has no post-Election-Day cure window. Note the statewide legal backdrop: under Pennsylvania Supreme Court rulings, mail ballots with a missing or incorrect handwritten date on the outer envelope are not counted — making the date field the most consequential thing to get right.
