Mail-ballot CURE policy

How Bucks notifies: "Notice and cure" — voters with a correctible defect are notified by postcard, phone, and/or email.

How to cure: In person at 55 E. Court Street, Doylestown, OR by voting a provisional ballot at the polls on Election Day.

Standard correctible defects: missing signature, missing date, naked ballot (missing inner secrecy envelope), or an improperly opened/sealed outer envelope.

Cure deadline: 8 PM on Election Day. Bucks does not offer a post–Election Day cure window. The scope of curing and undated/misdated ballots continues to be litigated statewide.
