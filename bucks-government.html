This Week at a Glance
  • Commissioner Bob Harvie (D) defeated Rep. Brian Fitzpatrick (R) in PA-01 — the AP called the 1st Congressional District race for Harvie at ~12:41 a.m. Wed, Nov 4, flipping a seat the GOP held for over a decade (Spotlight PA, WHYY).
  • Harvie's win opens a seat on the 3-member Board of Commissioners. Per Commissioner Marseglia, President Judge Raymond McHugh will fill it once the Bucks Democratic Party petitions the court; the appointee serves out Harvie's term through Jan 2028 (Bucks County Herald, Nov 4).
  • A visiting judge rejected Fitzpatrick's bid to remove Commissioner Diane Ellis-Marseglia (D) from the Board of Elections. Chester County Senior Judge Jeffrey Sommer heard the Nov 2 emergency petition (Bucks Judge McMaster recused) and let her stay for the Nov 3 vote (Phila. Inquirer; Bucks County Courier Times).
  • Common Pleas Judge Jordan Yeager will substitute for Harvie on the Board of Elections when it convenes (~one week post-election) to certify results, since Harvie was both a commissioner and a candidate (Bucks County Courier Times, Nov 2).
  • Democrats swept Bucks state-legislative races and the party captured a Harrisburg trifecta; the Beacon's live hub logged local House and Senate flips on election night (Bucks County Beacon, Nov 4).
  • Row offices & most judges: no office-specific news in the window — all 9 row offices remain Democratic, and the four judges sworn Jan 6, 2026 are settled on the bench.
  • Standing watch: the Marseglia/election-board fight is resolved for now; pollbook-pilot, climate-appeal, sheriff/ICE, treasurer-confirmation and register-of-wills items show no new movement this week.

Mail-ballot cure policy
How Bucks handles defective mail ballots Notice: Bucks operates "notice and cure" — voters with correctible defects are notified by postcard, phone, and email. How to cure: in person at 55 E. Court St., Doylestown, or vote provisionally at the polls. Correctible defects: missing signature, missing date, naked ballot (no secrecy envelope), improperly opened envelope. Deadline: 8 p.m. on Election Day — Bucks has no post-Election-Day cure window.
