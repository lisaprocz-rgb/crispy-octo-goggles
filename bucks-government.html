⭐ This Week at a Glance
This week at a glance:
  • Fitzpatrick sues to remove Marseglia from the election board. Rep. Brian Fitzpatrick (R) filed suit in Bucks County Court on Thursday, Oct. 29 seeking to bar Commissioner Diane Ellis-Marseglia (D) from the Board of Elections for the Nov. 3 count, arguing her partisanship violates his due-process rights. County Solicitor Dan Grieser had already refused the recusal demand on Oct. 28.
  • Judge Jordan Yeager now chairs the Board of Elections after Commissioner Bob Harvie (D) recused himself when he launched his congressional campaign — leaving Marseglia (D) and Gene DiGirolamo (R) as the two commissioner members.
  • PA-1 is a toss-up. A new Inquirer / New York Times / Siena poll (released Oct. 25) put Harvie ahead of Fitzpatrick 48%–46% — inside the margin of error.
  • Final-push weekend. Harvie held a 90-minute Levittown town hall; Gov. Josh Shapiro is set to rally with him in Doylestown Saturday (his third Bucks visit this month); Fitzpatrick leaned on labor support at a Northeast Philly union hall (Oct. 28 coverage).
  • Mail voting is well underway. As of Oct. 29, Bucks had sent 103,523 mail ballots (D 60,032 · R 30,598 · Other 12,893) and received 83,447 back — about an 81% return rate. Registration and mail-application deadlines have both passed; ballots are due by 8 p.m. Nov. 3.
  • Pollbook pilot stays off. Pennsylvania postponed its internet-connected e-pollbook pilot earlier in 2026; Bucks continues to run KnowInk Poll Pads on a closed network. No change this week.

Mail-ballot cure policy
How Bucks notifies votersBucks operates a "notice and cure" process: voters with a correctible defect are contacted by postcard, phone, and/or email so they can fix the ballot.
How to cureCure in person at the elections office, 55 E. Court St., Doylestown — or vote a provisional ballot at your polling place on Election Day.
Standard correctible defectsMissing signature, missing/incorrect date on the outer envelope, a "naked" ballot (missing secrecy envelope), or an improperly sealed envelope.
Cure deadline8 PM on Election Day (Nov. 3). Bucks offers no post-Election-Day cure window. Statewide litigation over undated/misdated mail ballots remains ongoing.
