A per-party mail-ballot snapshot (bucksdemocrats.org/mailballotstatus) was not published for the general election this run — see the Board of Elections section.
This Week at a Glance
  • Harvie goes to Washington. Vice-Chair Bob Harvie (D) defeated five-term Rep. Brian Fitzpatrick (R) in the PA-1 congressional race (AP call 12:41 a.m. Nov. 4). He is now Congressman-elect and sworn into the U.S. House Jan. 3, 2027.
  • A commissioner vacancy is coming. Because Harvie won a federal seat, the Bucks County Court of Common Pleas — not voters — will appoint a Democrat to serve the balance of his term (expires Jan. 2028). As of Nov. 6 the court had not announced an application process or timeline.
  • Judge rejected Fitzpatrick's election-board suit. Days before the election, a judge denied Brian Fitzpatrick's bid to remove Commissioner Diane Ellis-Marseglia (D) from the Board of Elections.
  • Clerk of Courts Eileen Hartnett Albillar (D) won State Senate SD-6, defeating incumbent Frank Farry (R) — setting up a second row-office vacancy when she takes the Harrisburg seat.
  • Blue trifecta. Bucks-area Democrats flipped PA House and Senate seats; statewide, Gov. Josh Shapiro won re-election and Democrats took the Harrisburg trifecta.
  • Courts on watch. Two men were charged in October in separate threat cases against five Bucks judges; the county's dismissed climate lawsuit is back before an appeals court.
  • Treasurer Mark Moffa remains acting (Senate confirmation still pending); the Register of Wills seat is still filled on an acting basis by First Deputy Douglas Wayne (D) after Linda Bobrin took a judgeship.

Mail-ballot cure policy
How Bucks notifies voters Bucks operates a "notice and cure" process: voters with a correctible defect are contacted by postcard, phone, and/or email.
How to cure Cure in person at the Board of Elections, 55 E. Court Street, Doylestown — or vote by provisional ballot at your polling place on Election Day.
Standard correctible defects Missing signature, missing date, "naked" ballot (no secrecy envelope), or an improperly opened/sealed outer envelope.
Cure deadline + litigation context The cure deadline is 8 p.m. on Election Day — Bucks offers no post-Election-Day cure window. The board's handling of undated/misdated and defective mail ballots has drawn repeated litigation, most recently Brian Fitzpatrick's unsuccessful suit over Ellis-Marseglia's board seat.
