Mail-Ballot Cure Policy
How Bucks notifiesBucks operates "notice and cure": voters with deficient ballots are notified by postcard, phone and email.
How to cureFix the ballot in person at the Board of Elections, 55 E. Court St., Doylestown — or vote by provisional ballot at your polling place on Election Day.
Standard correctible defectsMissing signature · missing date · "naked" ballot (no secrecy envelope) · improperly opened envelope.
Cure deadline + litigation contextCure deadline is 8 PM on Election Day — Bucks has no post-Election Day cure window. The date-on-envelope requirement remains in active state and federal litigation. Fresh this week:
  • Bucks County Beacon New Study Finds That Ballot Curing in Pennsylvania Helps More Mail Ballots Get Counted (June 4, 2026, by Carter Walker / Votebeat) — Penn study: voters told of a disqualifying error before Election Day were 25 points more likely to have their vote counted; ~20% of 2026 primary mail-ballot requestors gave no email address, so many can't be e-notified. Flags equal-protection variation between counties as the next legal frontier. Read more at the Bucks County Beacon → · Original at Votebeat →
