Mail-ballot cure policy

How Bucks notifies: "Notice and cure" — voters with a correctible defect are contacted by postcard, phone and/or email.

How to cure: In person at 55 E. Court St., Doylestown, OR vote a provisional ballot at the polls on Election Day.

Standard correctible defects: missing signature, missing date, naked ballot (no secrecy envelope), improperly opened/sealed envelope.

Cure deadline: 8 PM on Election Day. Bucks has no post-Election-Day cure window. Litigation over undated/wrongly-dated ballots remains an active statewide backdrop.
