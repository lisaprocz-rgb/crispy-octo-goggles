Mail-ballot cure policy
How Bucks handles defective mail ballots Bucks operates a "notice and cure" process for correctible mail-ballot defects.
  • How the county notifies voters: by postcard, phone, and email when a returned ballot has a correctible defect.
  • How to cure: in person at 55 E. Court Street, Doylestown, or by voting a provisional ballot at the polls.
  • Standard correctible defects: missing signature, missing date, naked ballot (no secrecy envelope), or an improperly opened/sealed envelope.
  • Cure deadline: 8 PM on Election Day. Bucks has no post-Election-Day cure window.

Litigation context: the handling of undated/misdated and provisional ballots has been repeatedly litigated in Bucks and statewide, and Commissioner Ellis-Marseglia's 2024 remarks on a disputed ballot vote are central to the election-board challenge described above.
