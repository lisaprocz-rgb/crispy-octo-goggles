This week at a glance:
  • Rep. Brian Fitzpatrick (R) sued Commissioner Diane Ellis-Marseglia (D) on Oct. 29 to remove her from the Bucks County Board of Elections, citing bias toward his opponent Bob Harvie. A hearing is set for 9 a.m. Monday, Nov. 2 in Common Pleas — one day before the election.
  • County Solicitor Dan Grieser rejected the recusal demand in an Oct. 28 letter, saying the Election Code only displaces a commissioner who is herself a candidate, and that Marseglia has certified Fitzpatrick's wins five times.
  • Gov. Josh Shapiro rallied in Doylestown on Oct. 31 for commissioner-turned-congressional-candidate Bob Harvie (D), who is challenging Fitzpatrick in PA-1.
  • DA Joe Khan announced new charges on Oct. 27 against an Edward A. Eisenhard in an assault case.
  • Board of Elections: on-demand voting and ballot drop boxes are open at seven staffed sites; mail-ballot application deadline (Oct. 27) has passed; completed ballots are due by 8 p.m. Nov. 3.
  • Sheriff Danny Ceisler (D) remains in the news for his Workers Circle pledge (Oct. 8) not to allow ICE at polling places.
  • All three commissioners sit ex officio on the Board of Elections; Harvie chairs it. The Fitzpatrick suit targets its composition heading into Election Day.

Mail-ballot cure policy

Bucks operates "notice and cure": voters with correctable defects are notified by postcard, phone, and/or email.

  • How to cure: in person at 55 E. Court St., Doylestown, OR by voting a provisional ballot at the polls.
  • Cure deadline: 8 p.m. on Election Day — no post-Election-Day cure window in Bucks.
  • Standard correctible defects: missing signature, missing date, naked ballot, improperly opened envelope.
  • Litigation context: undated/misdated-ballot handling is at the center of the Fitzpatrick v. Marseglia suit; her 2024 comments on this issue are cited in the complaint (hearing Nov. 2).
