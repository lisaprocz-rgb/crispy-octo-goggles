Mail-ballot CURE policy
How Bucks notifies a voter of a defect Bucks operates "notice and cure" — voters with curable defects get a postcard, phone call, and email from the BOE. New for 2026: outer envelopes have a small viewing hole and the inner secrecy envelope is colored yellow, so staff can spot "naked ballots" without opening anything.
How to cure In person at 55 E. Court Street, Doylestown, OR by going to the polls and voting a provisional ballot.
Standard correctible defects Missing voter signature, missing date, "naked" ballot (no secrecy envelope), or improperly opened outer envelope. Unsigned and undated ballots are not counted absent cure.
Cure deadline (and the litigation context) Bucks's cure window closes 8 PM Election Day — there is no post-Election Day cure period in Bucks. The PA Supreme Court has repeatedly upheld that undated and unsigned ballots may not be counted, including in the November 2024 Marseglia-controversy ruling.
