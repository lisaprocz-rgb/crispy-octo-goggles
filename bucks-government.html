This Week at a Glance
This week at a glance:
  • Vice Chair Bob Harvie (D) won the PA-01 U.S. House seat on Nov 3, ousting five-term Republican Rep. Brian Fitzpatrick — the AP called it in the early hours of Nov 4.
  • Harvie's win creates a vacancy on the 3-member Board of Commissioners. Per Commissioner Diane Marseglia, President Judge Raymond McHugh will fill it after the Bucks County Democratic Party files a petition; the appointee serves through January 2028. BCDC Chair Steve Santarsiero said a replacement had "not yet" been chosen.
  • Clerk of Courts Eileen Hartnett Albillar (D) won PA Senate District 6, defeating Republican incumbent Frank Farry — flipping the seat and foreshadowing a second row-office vacancy when she is sworn to the Senate.
  • Gov. Josh Shapiro (D) was reelected statewide on Nov 3, part of what the Bucks County Beacon billed as a "Blue Trifecta in Harrisburg."
  • The Board of Elections is canvassing the Nov 3 results; official certification is pending. Check the CivicClerk portal for canvass-meeting dates.
  • No fresh developments surfaced this week on the acting Treasurer and Register of Wills vacancies, the pollbook pilot, the climate-suit appeal, or the Ceisler/ICE storyline — each is held open under Standing Watch below.

Mail-ballot cure policy
How Bucks notifies: "notice and cure" — voters with a correctible defect are contacted by postcard, phone, and email.

How to cure: in person at 55 E. Court St., Doylestown, or by voting a provisional ballot at the polls.

Standard correctible defects: missing signature, missing date, naked ballot (no secrecy envelope), improperly opened envelope.

Cure deadline: 8:00 PM on Election Day — Bucks has no post-Election-Day cure window. Undated/mis-dated-ballot treatment remains the subject of ongoing statewide litigation.
