Mail-ballot cure policy

How Bucks notifies: "notice and cure" — voters with a correctible defect are contacted by postcard, phone and/or email.

How to cure: in person at 55 E. Court St., Doylestown — OR vote a provisional ballot at the polls.

Standard correctible defects: missing signature, missing date, naked ballot (no secrecy envelope), or an improperly opened envelope.

Cure deadline: 8:00 PM on Election Day. Bucks has no post-Election-Day cure window. (The undated/incorrectly-dated outer-envelope question remains the subject of ongoing statewide litigation.)
