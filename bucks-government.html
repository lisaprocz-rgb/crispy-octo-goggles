Mail-ballot "notice and cure" policy
How Bucks notifies votersBucks operates a notice-and-cure process: voters whose mail ballots have a correctable defect are notified by postcard, phone, and/or email.
How to cureCure in person at the Board of Elections, 55 E. Court St., Doylestown — or vote a provisional ballot at the polls on Election Day.
Standard correctible defectsMissing signature · missing handwritten date · "naked" ballot (no secrecy envelope) · improperly opened/sealed outer envelope.
Cure deadlineThe cure deadline is 8 p.m. on Election Day — Bucks offers no post-Election-Day cure window. The handwritten-date / undated-ballot question remains the subject of ongoing statewide litigation.
