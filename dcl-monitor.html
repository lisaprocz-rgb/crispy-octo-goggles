Announced Fall 2027 itineraries (Sept–Nov 2027)
Disney Wish — New York Sails from NYC to Bermuda (5 sailings, with extended stays) and a New England / Canada Atlantic-coast route — the first NE/Canada season in nearly four years. 4- to 7-night cruises from late Sept 2027. New homeport
Disney Wish — Port Canaveral After the NY season, 3- & 4-night Bahamian cruises from Port Canaveral, visiting Disney Castaway Cay and Disney Lookout Cay.
Disney Destiny For the first time, homeports in Port Canaveral to open the season, then continues from Fort Lauderdale. 3- to 7-night cruises to the Bahamas & Western Caribbean. First PC homeport
Disney Treasure — Port Canaveral Eastern & Western Caribbean sailings, plus a special six-night Bahamian voyage with two stops at Disney Castaway Cay.
Disney Dream 3- & 4-night Bahamas sailings from Port Canaveral (Sept–Nov 2027) and Fort Lauderdale (Nov 2027), including one six-night sailing with two Castaway Cay stops.
Disney Fantasy Bahamas, Western Caribbean and Bermuda from Port Canaveral (Sept–Nov) and Fort Lauderdale — including an eight-night Bermuda cruise for the first time. 8-night Bermuda debut
Disney Magic Baja / Mexican Riviera from San Diego through early Nov 2027, two Panama Canal repositioning cruises (14 nights, new ports Fuerte Amador & Colón), then Bahamian itineraries from Galveston.
Disney Wonder 3- to 7-night cruises from San Diego to Baja, the Mexican Riviera, Ensenada, Cabo San Lucas and Catalina Island across the Fall 2027 & Spring 2028 seasons.
Disney Adventure — Singapore New 3- & 4-night Southeast Asia itineraries from Singapore (Sept–Dec 2027), adding a new port: Langkawi, Malaysia.
