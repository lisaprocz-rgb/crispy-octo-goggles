What's confirmed for Fall 2027 (Sept–Nov 2027):
  • Disney Destiny — home ports at Port Canaveral for the first time; 3- to 8-night Bahamas & Western Caribbean (opens the season in Fort Lauderdale first).
  • Disney Wish — arrives in New York City late Sept 2027; 4- to 7-night sailings incl. Bermuda (5 sailings) and a return to New England & Canada.
  • Disney Fantasy & Disney Wish — 5- to 7-night Southern & Eastern Caribbean.
  • Disney Magic — two Panama Canal repositioning cruises; San Diego Baja/Mexican Riviera.
  • Disney Wonder — sails from San Diego (Baja/Mexican Riviera) through early Nov 2027.
  • Disney Treasure — Port Canaveral; new Panama ports (Fuerte Amador, Colón).
  • Disney Believe — sails late 2027, but its specific itineraries have not yet been announced.
  • Booking: early access for Castaway Club opened Aug 31, 2026; general booking opens Tuesday, Sept 8, 2026 on disneycruise.com.
