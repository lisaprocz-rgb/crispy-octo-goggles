🚨 Top Corporate Story: FCC Targets Disney's ABC License Renewals

Washington Post · FCC orders early license renewals for Disney's ABC stations after Trump's Kimmel criticism (Apr 28)

The FCC has directed Disney to file early license renewals within 30 days for the eight ABC-owned-and-operated TV stations in cities including New York, Chicago, and Philadelphia — licenses that weren't otherwise due for renewal for years. The move follows President Trump's call for Jimmy Kimmel to be fired over a joke about Melania Trump.

CBS News · FCC frames probe around Disney's diversity practices (Apr 28)

Officially, the agency says it is investigating Disney's ABC stations for "possible violations of the Communications Act of 1934 and the FCC's rules, including the agency's prohibition on unlawful discrimination" — tying the probe to DEI rather than Kimmel directly.

Time / Axios · FCC commissioner calls move "unprecedented, unlawful, and going nowhere" (Apr 28)

FCC commissioner Anna M. Gomez slammed the directive as a "political stunt." Most legal experts agree ABC's licenses are not in immediate danger — the FCC cannot revoke them unilaterally; any case would ultimately go before an administrative law judge. Disney has filed a defense of its compliance record.

Walt Disney Company · Corporate & Business

Leadership & Layoffs

Bloomberg · Disney to cut 1,000 jobs in first layoffs under CEO Josh D'Amaro (Apr 14)

The Walt Disney Company is eliminating up to 1,000 positions in the first major workforce reduction under new CEO Josh D'Amaro, who took over from Bob Iger on March 18. Cuts are tied to an internal restructuring effort dubbed "Project Imagine," consolidating the marketing organization under chief marketing and brand officer Asad Ayaz.

CNBC / WSJ · Cuts span marketing, studios, TV, ESPN, and corporate teams (Apr 9)

While the bulk of layoffs land in the recently consolidated marketing department, the reduction also touches studio arms, TV divisions, ESPN, product/technology teams, and certain corporate functions. Iger remains a Senior Advisor and board member through Dec 31, 2026.
