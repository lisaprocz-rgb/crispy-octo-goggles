🚨 Breaking: Disney Layoffs & Leadership Shakeup

Bloomberg — Disney to Cut 1,000 Jobs in First Layoffs Under CEO Josh D'Amaro (Apr 14, 2026) Walt Disney Co. is eliminating roughly 1,000 jobs as new CEO Josh D'Amaro begins organizational changes, about five weeks after he officially took over from Bob Iger at the March 18 shareholder meeting. Cuts are spread across studio & television, ESPN, Product & Technology, and corporate functions, with marketing hit particularly hard under new Chief Marketing & Brand Officer Asad Ayaz.
Deadline — CEO Josh D'Amaro Confirms Layoffs in Marketing & Brand Group (Apr 2026) In a company memo, D'Amaro wrote that Disney must "constantly assess how to foster a more agile and technologically-enabled workforce to meet tomorrow's needs." The consolidation rolls up film, TV, and streaming marketing under Ayaz, who was promoted in January, eliminating duplication across segments.
The Wrap / WDWNT — Entire Teams Eliminated, Including Home Entertainment & EPK (Apr 2026) The home entertainment team and electronic press kit (EPK) teams were wiped out entirely, along with specialty publicity divisions. Among high-profile departures is Dustin Sandoval, SVP Global Digital Marketing, who led campaigns for Avatar: The Way of Water and Avengers: Endgame. Severance details have been reported by WDWNT.
CNBC / Variety — Context: Cuts Are a Fraction of the Iger-Era Reductions (Apr 2026) While noteworthy as D'Amaro's first major move, these 1,000 cuts are far smaller than the roughly 8,000 positions Iger eliminated between 2023–2025 that delivered $7.5 billion in cost savings. D'Amaro's memo acknowledged the difficulty: "I know this is hard."
