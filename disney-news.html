🚨 Major Corporate News — D'Amaro Era Takes Shape

The first sweeping layoffs of the Josh D'Amaro era are still reverberating a week later. Roughly 1,000 jobs have been eliminated across studios, ESPN, marketing, product & technology, and corporate functions. The home entertainment division — in place since 1987 as Buena Vista Home Video — was wiped out entirely, and nearly all of Marvel Studios' visual development team is gone.

Layoffs Under New CEO

Bloomberg · Disney to Cut 1,000 Jobs in First Layoffs Under CEO Josh D'Amaro (Apr 14, 2026)
D'Amaro, who officially took over at the March 18 shareholder meeting ending Bob Iger's second stint, informed staff by memo on the morning of April 14. Cuts span studios, television, ESPN, product & technology and corporate teams, and are tied to an internal restructuring program called "Project Imagine."
Deadline · D'Amaro Confirms Layoffs in Marketing and Brand Group (Apr 2026)
A follow-on memo confirmed the marketing and brand organization — led by Chief Marketing and Brand Officer Asad Ayaz — is bearing a disproportionate share of the cuts. Senior departures include Dustin Sandoval, SVP of Global Digital Marketing, who oversaw "Avatar: The Way of Water" and "Avengers: Endgame" campaigns.

Home Entertainment Division Shuttered

MickeyBlog / CBR / ScreenRant · Disney's Entire Home Entertainment Division Eliminated After 39 Years (Apr 16, 2026)
Every remaining member of the team led by Chris Bess was let go, ending a unit that traced back to 1987's Buena Vista Home Video. Disney's existing manufacturing-and-distribution deal with Sony covers pressing discs, but with the creative-control team gone the future of Disney's DVD, Blu-ray, 4K UHD, and Steelbook releases is genuinely uncertain.

Marvel Visual Development Gutted

Variety · Marvel Visual Director Andy Park Out After 16 Years (Apr 2026)
Marvel Studios' visual development team shrank from 13–15 artists to a skeleton crew, with outside contractors now expected to be hired per project. Park led the team that produced first-pass character designs, costumes, and shot looks for virtually every MCU project of the past decade and a half.
Gizmodo · Former Marvel Art Staff Call Cuts "Shortsighted" (Apr 2026)
Laid-off artists argue the in-house vis-dev group was a key reason the MCU maintained any house style. Industry reporters note the move could either fix or worsen Marvel's "same look" problem depending on how contractor relationships shake out.
