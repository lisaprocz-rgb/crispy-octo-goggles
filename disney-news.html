🚨 Corporate & Business — Breaking

Variety / NPR / CNBC · FCC orders early license renewal for 8 ABC-owned stations (Apr 28, 2026)
FCC Chairman Brendan Carr ordered Disney's eight ABC-owned broadcast stations to file license renewals within 30 days (by May 28), citing a probe into the company's DEI practices. The action follows President Trump's renewed criticism of Jimmy Kimmel after a Melania Trump joke at the White House Correspondents' Dinner. Commissioner Anna Gomez called it "the most egregious action this FCC has taken in violation of the First Amendment to date." Disney says ABC stations are "in full compliance with FCC rules" and is preparing to defend through legal channels.
Bloomberg / Variety / Deadline · Disney layoffs of ~1,000 employees underway under new CEO Josh D'Amaro (Apr 14, 2026)
D'Amaro confirmed cuts in a memo ("I know this is hard"), with the layoffs concentrated in marketing and brand under newly elevated CMO Asad Ayaz, plus impacts across studio, TV, ESPN, Product & Technology, and corporate functions. It's the first major restructuring action since D'Amaro took over from Bob Iger on March 18. Disney has also abandoned its previously announced ESPN spinoff plan, positioning sports at the core of its streaming future.
