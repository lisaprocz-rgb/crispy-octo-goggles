🚨 Breaking Corporate News

Disney Begins Layoffs of ~1,000 Employees Under New CEO Josh D'Amaro

Variety / Deadline / CNBC — April 14, 2026

Disney is cutting approximately 1,000 roles as part of a marketing restructuring under new CEO Josh D'Amaro, who took the helm on March 18. In a memo to employees today, D'Amaro wrote "I know this is hard," confirming the layoffs are underway. Cuts span marketing functions across Disney's studios, TV networks, ESPN, Product & Technology, and corporate groups — primarily driven by the consolidation of all marketing into a new enterprise-wide division under Chief Marketing Officer Asad Ayaz. This is the first significant restructuring action under D'Amaro's tenure.

Sources: Variety · Deadline · CNBC

Josh D'Amaro Officially Takes the Disney CEO Throne — Bob Iger's 52-Year Era Ends

Variety — March 18, 2026

Josh D'Amaro was officially named CEO on February 3 and assumed the role on March 18 at Disney's annual shareholder meeting, ending Bob Iger's run after 52 years and two stints as CEO. D'Amaro, who previously led Disney Experiences (the parks and resorts division), succeeded Iger after a period of significant uncertainty that included an extended succession race and a major turnaround of the business.

Source: Variety

CNBC / Yahoo Finance — April 2026

Despite reporting a strong Q1 2026 earnings beat (EPS $1.63 vs. $1.57 expected; revenue $25.98B vs. $25.62B expected), Disney's stock has fallen roughly 16% year-to-date, trading around $98–100.
