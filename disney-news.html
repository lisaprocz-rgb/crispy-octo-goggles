📊 Breaking This Morning
Disney Q2 FY26 Earnings: Revenue +7%, Streaming Operating Income +88%
Disney posted Q2 results this morning, May 6. Revenue rose 7% YoY to $25.2B, beating Wall Street's $24.9B consensus. Adjusted EPS of $1.57 also topped the $1.50 estimate. Disney+ / Hulu revenue grew 13% to $5.49B, and the streaming unit's operating income soared 88% to $582M — the first quarter the entertainment streaming business cleared a double-digit operating margin (10.6%). It's the first earnings report under new CEO Josh D'Amaro.
