Disney CEO Josh D'Amaro — 1,000-Person Layoffs Confirmed

Variety / Bloomberg / Deadline   Disney Cuts 1,000 Jobs in First Major Restructuring Under New CEO (Apr 14–15, 2026)
Josh D'Amaro, who became Disney CEO on March 18, sent an internal memo to employees on April 14 confirming layoffs affecting approximately 1,000 positions across the company. D'Amaro called it "hard and difficult news" and framed the cuts as necessary to streamline a newly consolidated enterprise marketing division under Chief Marketing & Brand Officer Asad Ayaz. The reductions primarily hit marketing, publicity, and awards divisions — including the entire home entertainment team and the full electronic press kit group — with roughly 20 publicity employees also impacted. Hulu, FX, ESPN, ABC News, Marvel, and corporate technology/finance teams were all affected.

Hollywood Reporter / The Wrap   Entire Teams Eliminated — Senior Executives Depart (Apr 15, 2026)
Among the notable departures is Dustin Sandoval, SVP of Global Digital Marketing, who oversaw campaigns for "Avatar: The Way of Water" and "Avengers: Endgame." Marvel's entire publicity team was also eliminated. This marks the first significant workforce reduction under D'Amaro and follows the ~8,000 cuts made during Bob Iger's second tenure that saved approximately $7.5 billion.
